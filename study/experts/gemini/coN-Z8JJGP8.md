## Point-by-point
- Siemens Energy reported strong Q4 results driven by higher export contribution.
- New order wins increased by 92.1% Year-on-Year (YoY).
- Q4 revenue grew 30.5% YoY to ₹3,453 crore (vs ₹2,646 crore YoY), taking annual revenue past the ₹10,000 crore milestone.
- EBITDA increased 71.5% YoY to ₹823 crore (vs ₹480 crore YoY), with EBITDA margins expanding to 23.8% from 18.1%.
- Net Profit rose 81.4% YoY to ₹653 crore (vs ₹360 crore YoY).
- Company announced ₹2,060 crore capex to add 30,000 MVA transformer capacity, alongside ongoing capex at Kalwa (power transformers) and Sambhajinagar (high-voltage switchgear) facilities.
