## Point-by-point
- Motilal Oswal ki 30th Annual Wealth Creation Study (2020-2025) report me Indian market ke 17 saal ke data (2008 se 2025 - NTD Era) aur aage ke 17 saal (2025 se 2042 - MTD Era) ke आधार par quantitative filters aur stock screening rules diye gaye hain.
- NTD Era (2008-2025) me top 500 me se sirf 35 companies hi true 'Compounders' ban paayein jinone 17-saal me 23% average price CAGR return diya.
- Report me Wealth Effect macro model diya gaya hai: Jab share bazaar ka market cap badhta hai to upper/middle class ka spending confidence badhta hai. Delta Market Cap ka 5% additional consumption spending me jata hai jo GDP growth ko 0.4% boost deta hai.
- PFCE (Private Final Consumption Expenditure) analysis ke mutabiq Durables (Cars, 2-Wheelers, White Goods) ka share 3% se badhkar 10% hoga (20% CAGR), jabki Necessities (FMCG, Food) ka growth rate ~8% par simit rahega.
- Capital Market intermediaries (Exchanges, AMCs, Broking) asset-light models hain jo Demat account J-curve acceleration (>20% YoY) aur MF Equity AUM CAGR (>18%) ke dauran high RoE aur high dividend payout pradan karte hain.
