## Point-by-point
- Video analyzes 3 specific technical breakout setups on Indian stocks (Bank of Maharashtra, Dr. Agarwal's Healthcare, and International Gemological Institute).
- Setup 1 (Multi-Year Consolidation ATH Breakout): Bank of Maharashtra on 1-Month timeframe breaking out of a 19-year consolidation to form a new All-Time High. Stop loss/invalidation defined below 78.
- Setup 2 (Failed Breakout Recovery & Re-Breakout): Dr. Agarwal's Healthcare on 1-Week timeframe. Corrected 30% from ATH, attempted breakout earlier and failed, then built a base and broke out again strongly above ATH. Invalidation defined below 509.
- Setup 3 (Range Reversal Breakout with Retest Filter): International Gemological Institute (IGI) on 1-Week timeframe. Consolidated in a range for ~1.5 years before giving a reversal breakout. Because steep momentum preceded the breakout, a retest of the breakout level or brief consolidation near breakout is expected before continuation. Invalidation defined below 390.

## Niyam
- [new/yes] Multi-Year Consolidation All-Time High Breakout Strategy (Monthly Timeframe) | entry: Buy when stock price closes above multi-year (e.g., 10+ year) consolidation resistance level, marking a fresh All-Time High. | exit: Exit when price closes below the invalidation support level. | stop: Below recent consolidation breakout support (e.g., 78 in Bank of Maharashtra example).
- [new/yes] Failed Breakout Recovery & ATH Re-Breakout Strategy (Weekly Timeframe) | entry: Buy on a strong weekly close above prior All-Time High resistance after stock rebounds from a ~30% drawdown and absorbs a previous failed breakout attempt. | exit: Exit when price falls below the swing low / invalidation key support. | stop: Below recent swing low consolidation level (e.g., 509 in Dr. Agarwal's Healthcare example).
- [new/yes] 1.5-Year Range Reversal Breakout with Pre-Breakout Extension Retest Rule | entry: Buy on range breakout confirmation, or wait for retest/consolidation at breakout level if price rallied steeply immediately before the breakout. | exit: Exit when price falls below range support level. | stop: Below base consolidation support level (e.g., 390 in IGI example).
