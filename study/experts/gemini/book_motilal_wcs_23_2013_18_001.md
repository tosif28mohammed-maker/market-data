## Point-by-point
- HDFC Bank emerged as the biggest wealth creator (INR 3,247 billion created) for 2013-18, dethroning TCS and breaking TCS's 5-year streak at rank 1.
- Indiabulls Ventures was the fastest wealth creator over 2013-18 with a 30x price multiplier (97% CAGR). 8 of the top 10 fastest wealth creators had a base market cap < INR 20 billion (Rs 2,000 Crore), and 5 of them traded at single-digit base P/E (<10x).
- Titan Company was named the most consistent wealth creator, appearing in all top 100 lists for 10 consecutive study periods with a 10-year price CAGR of 33%. 100% of the top 10 consistent wealth creators were consumer-facing businesses.
- PSU wealth creation remains low (9% of total), but PSUs facing minimal private sector competition (monopolistic PSUs like OMCs, Power Grid, GAIL, IGL, BEL, NBCC) created wealth, whereas competitive PSUs (especially PSU banks like Bank of India, PNB, and BHEL) dominated wealth destruction.
- Market cap distribution over 20 years (1998-2018) shows continuous value migration: Private Indian entrepreneurs' share rose from 41% to 73%, PSUs dropped from 36% to 15%, and MNCs dropped from 23% to 12%.
- Market Cap Crossovers: Mid-to-Mega (stocks ranked 101-300 moving to top 100) generated 43% average price CAGR over 5 years (16 out of 200 stocks made the transition). Mini-to-Mid (ranked >300 moving to 101-300) generated 61% average CAGR (46 stocks).
- Valuation Screen Performance: Payback Ratio < 1x delivered 39% price CAGR; PEG < 0.5x delivered 38% price CAGR; Base P/E < 10x delivered 33% price CAGR; Base P/B < 1x delivered 37% price CAGR.
