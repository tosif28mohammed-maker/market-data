## Point-by-point
- HDFC Group Case Study: HDFC Bank has a 20-year CAP (RoE > 15% every single year from 1998 to 2017). Both HDFC and HDFC Bank achieved a 20-year GAP (rolling 5-year PAT CAGR > 15% every year from 1997 to 2017).
- Financial Services 90% Rule: In financial services, the single most critical factor for success is 'Underwrite well'. Investors must verify management's adherence to sound underwriting.
- Capital Allocation Filter: Capital misallocation breaks CAP and GAP. Investors must exit long positions on the first sign of capital misallocation.
- CAP Definition: Competition Advantage Period is the timeframe where Return on Equity (RoE) > Cost of Equity. Quantified as at least 5 consecutive years of RoE > 15%.
- GAP Definition: Growth Advantage Period is the timeframe where earnings growth exceeds benchmark indices. Quantified as at least 5 consecutive years of rolling PAT CAGR > 15% with zero YoY PAT drops.
- Fastest Wealth Creators Pattern: Small base size (Market Cap < Rs 2,000 Crore / INR 20 Billion), scalable industry/niche, single-digit base P/E (< 10x), and PAT CAGR > 30% produced a 73% CAGR return.
- Consistent Wealth Creators Pattern: 8 out of top 10 consistent wealth creators are consumer-facing with strong discretionary brands (Asian Paints, Titan, etc.). Staples/basic goods (Colgate) lack steady high growth, while discretionary brands sustain long-term wealth creation.
- Market Cap Crossovers: Mini-to-Mid (Rank > 300 to Rank 101-300) yielded 54% average return CAGR (49 companies). Mid-to-Mega (Rank 101-300 to Top 100) yielded 40% average return CAGR (18 companies).
- Wealth Destruction in Cyclicals: Metals/Mining, Real Estate, Capital Goods, and PSU Banks accounted for maximum wealth destruction. The only safe way to trade cyclicals is buying cheap during severe downcycles.

## Niyam
- [twist/yes] Select companies with 20 consecutive years of RoE > 15% (20-Year CAP) AND 20 consecutive years of rolling 5-year PAT CAGR > 15% (20-Year GAP) | entry: Buy companies in financial/consumer sectors that have completed 5+ years of RoE > 15% and rolling 5-year PAT CAGR > 15% | exit: Exit on the first signs of capital misallocation or if RoE falls below 15% | stop: Annual RoE < 15% or 5-year rolling PAT CAGR < 15%
- [twist/yes] GAP Longevity Filter with Strict Non-Declining PAT Rule: Select stocks with rolling 5-year PAT CAGR > 15% where no single year's PAT is lower than the previous year (PAT_t >= PAT_{t-1}) | entry: Buy when rolling 5-year PAT CAGR > 15% AND annual PAT has increased YoY for 5 consecutive years | exit: Exit immediately if annual PAT declines YoY (PAT_t < PAT_{t-1}) or rolling 5-year PAT CAGR drops below 15% | stop: Any single year YoY PAT decline (PAT_t < PAT_{t-1})
- [twist/yes] Consumer Discretionary Brand Consistency Filter: Prefer Consumer Discretionary brand leaders over Consumer Staples for long-term compounding, requiring 10-year PAT CAGR > 15% and 10-year Price CAGR > 20% | entry: Buy market leaders in consumer discretionary (paints, jewellery, auto, retail) with 10-year average RoE > 20% and 10-year PAT CAGR > 15% | exit: Exit if 10-year PAT CAGR falls below 12% or pricing power/brand preference weakens | stop: RoE < 15% or annual PAT growth < 10%
- [twist/yes] Fastest Wealth Creator Small-Cap Scalability Screen: Buy stocks with Base Market Cap < Rs 2,000 Crore (INR 20 Billion), base P/E < 10x, operating in scalable sectors, with 5-year PAT CAGR > 30% | entry: Buy when Market Cap < Rs 2,000 Crore, Trailing P/E < 10x (or single digit P/E), operating in scalable sector (Pharma, Auto, Niche Retail/Consumer), and historical/projected 5-year PAT CAGR > 30% | exit: Exit after 5 years or when P/E expands above 35x / PAT growth slows below 15% | stop: 5-year PAT CAGR falls below 15%
