## Point-by-point
- 15th Annual Wealth Creation Study (2005-2010) by Motilal Oswal highlights key quantitative metrics driving 5-year multibaggers and introduces 'UU (Unknown & Unknowable) Investing'.
- Payback Ratio (Market Cap / Sum of estimated PAT over next 5 years) < 1.0x proved to be the most consistent standalone valuation metric for high returns. Payback Ratio < 0.5x delivered 61% price CAGR.
- Traditional valuation screens (P/E < 10x, P/B < 1x, P/S < 1x) failed to guarantee superior returns during 2005-2010, whereas earnings growth and Payback Ratio dominated.
- Companies with high earnings growth (PAT CAGR > 30% to > 70%) delivered 37% to 50% Price CAGR, successfully overriding initial high P/E valuation de-ratings.
- Small-to-mid base market cap (< Rs 50 billion / Rs 5,000 crore) significantly outperformed large caps in both PAT CAGR and Price CAGR.
- Companies with low initial RoE (< 5%) delivered highest returns (46% Price CAGR, 65% PAT CAGR) as they transitioned from investment/cyclical trough to RoE expansion.
