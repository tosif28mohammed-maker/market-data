## Point-by-point
- Indian Railways ne haal hi me 9,638 wagons ke contracts add kiye hain jinki kul value Rs 3,925 crore hai (Jupiter Wagons: Rs 2,026 Cr / 4,955 wagons, Titagarh: Rs 1,051 Cr / 2,583 wagons, Texmaco: Rs 412 Cr / 1,020 wagons).
- Ye stocks apne all-time high se 60% se zyada gir chuke hain kyonki FY26 me wheelset shortage, delayed deliveries aur fewer tenders ki wajah se sales me kami aayi thi.
- Order Book to Sales metric: Jupiter Wagons ki order book Rs 5,850 Cr (201% of FY26 sales), Titagarh Rail Rs 28,695 Cr (901% of FY26 sales), Texmaco Rail Rs 10,940 Cr (250% of FY26 sales) hai.
- Q2 FY27 YoY sales Jupiter (-2%), Titagarh (-1%) aur Texmaco (-31%) me girawat dekhi gayi, jabki QoQ sabhi me sudhar (+15%, +4%, +15%) tha.
- Valuations: Jupiter 47x PE aur Titagarh 45x PE par demanding hain, jabki Texmaco 17x PE par one-time gain se affected hai.
- Kamyabi ke liye aane wale quarters me order execution rate, profit margins aur cash flow ko track karne ki salah di gayi hai.
