## Point-by-point
- India Multi-Trillion Dollar (MTD) Era (2025-2042) me India ki GDP $4 Trillion se $16 Trillion hone ka anuman hai, jisse Financials, Consumer Discretionary, Autos, Realty aur Healthcare me multi-year compounding growth ki sambhavna hai.
- India MTD Portfolio construction ke liye Top 500 Market Cap universe me se screening ke 6 quantitative-cum-qualitative steps bataye gaye hain.
- Business Quality Filter: RoE > 12% (Cost of Equity threshold 12% ke upar होना zaruri hai).
- Earnings Growth Filter: 3-Year PAT CAGR (2022-2025) >= 20% hona chahiye (MTD era target return threshold 20%).
- Price Valuation Filter: Trailing PEG (2025 P/E / 2022-25 PAT CAGR) < 2.0x hona chahiye.
- Market Leadership Filter: Company ko apne respective sector me Top-3 market leader position me hona chahiye.
- DCF Longevity P/E Model: Explicit high-growth (20% PAT CAGR, 25% RoE) horizon 5 saal se badhakar 10 saal karne par fair payable P/E 18x se badhakar 23x (31% upside) aur 15 saal karne par 30x (67% upside) ho jata hai.
