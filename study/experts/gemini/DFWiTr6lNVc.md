# Ather Energy vs Aditya Birla Real Estate: Sustainable vs Event-Driven Turnaround Analysis
स्रोत: transcript: Tosif laptop · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DFWiTr6lNVc · Gemini ने पढ़ा 2026-11-27 19:41 UTC

## Point-by-point
- Ather Energy ne Q2 me 133 crore ke operating loss ko 11.6 crore ke operating profit me badla, jo high sales volume aur operational efficiency ke kaaran gradual tarike se aaya.
- Aditya Birla Real Estate (ABRE) ne 74 crore ke operating loss ko 158 crore ke operating profit me badla, lekin yeh 2 completed projects ki revenue recognition se hua event-driven turnaround tha.
- Headline operating profit dekhne ke bajaye turnaround ka source check karein: project completion/one-off event turnaround ke bajaye volume growth aur operating efficiency wale turnarounds zyada sustainable hote hain.

## Niyam
- [twist/partly] Loss-to-Profit (L-to-P) Sustainability Filter: Real estate project completion ya one-off event-driven operating profit turnaround ke bajaye sirf volume expansion aur operational efficiency se hue L-to-P turnarounds ko buy karein. | entry: Jab company negative EBITDA se positive EBITDA me shift ho aur yeh improvement gradual sales volume expansion & operational efficiency se driven ho | exit: number nahi diya | stop: number nahi diya
