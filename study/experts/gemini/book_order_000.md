## Point-by-point
- Motilal Oswal 8th Wealth Creation Study (2003) aur baad ki reports me wealth creation ke main drivers ko identify kiya gaya: Earnings Growth (PAT growth) aur P/E Re-rating.
- PEG (Price Earnings to Growth) Ratio < 1.0 hyper wealth creators ko filter karne ke liye sabse prabhavi metric hai.
- Value Migration rule: Purane/inefficient business models se naye efficient models ki taraf capital shift hone par sector leaders 5-10 saal tak outperformance dete hain.
- Demonetisation aur macro shocks ke baad cash-heavy sectors (Realty, Auto, Consumer Durables) me short-term alpha negative hota hai jabki IT/Pharma/Private Banks resilient rehte hain.
