# Engulfing Candlestick Trading Strategy with Support-Resistance and Volume Confirmation
स्रोत: Dhan (YouTube) · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gRr6V7zfbAA · Gemini ने पढ़ा 2026-11-25 09:43 UTC

## Point-by-point
- Engulfing pattern sabse clear aur powerful reversal signal deta hai.
- Bullish Engulfing Pattern: Pehli candlestick chhoti red hoti hai aur agli candle long green candle hoti hai jo pehli red candle ko poori tarah engulf (cover) kar leti hai. Yeh buyers ke dominance ko darshata hai.
- Bearish Engulfing Pattern: Pehli candlestick chhoti green hoti hai aur agli candle long red candle hoti hai jo pehli green candle ko poori tarah engulf kar leti hai. Yeh sellers ki strength ko darshata hai.
- Location Filter: Bullish Engulfing pattern ko strong support zone par dhoondhna chahiye. Bearish Engulfing pattern ko strong resistance zone par dhoondhna chahiye.
- Volume Confirmation Filter: Har engulfing trade me volume confirmation zaroori hai. Agar support par Bullish Engulfing ya resistance par Bearish Engulfing bane lekin volume kam (Low Volume) ho, to trade ko avoid karna chahiye. Pattern + High Volume hi entry signal hota hai.
