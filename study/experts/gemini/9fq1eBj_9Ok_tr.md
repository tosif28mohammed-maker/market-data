# Triple EMA + Stochastic RSI + Rising ADX Multi-Timeframe Swing Strategy
स्रोत: transcript: Tosif laptop · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9fq1eBj_9Ok · Gemini ने पढ़ा 2026-11-27 18:45 UTC

## Point-by-point
- Yeh ek mechanical momentum swing trading strategy hai jisme 3 indicators ka confluence use hota hai: Triple Moving Average, Stochastic RSI, aur ADX.
- Trend Filter: Triple Moving Average (SMA ya EMA) 1:3:9 ke ratio me set honi chahiye (jaise 5-15-50 ya conservative traders ke liye 9-30-100). Setup buy side ke liye tabhi valid hai jab moving averages bullish alignment me hon.
- Trigger Filter: Stochastic RSI overbought zone se nahi, balki strictly oversold territory (< 20) se upar aakar bullish buy crossover de chuka ho.
- Momentum Filter: ADX (Average Directional Index) ka slope upward (rising) hona chahiye. Video ke anusaar ADX > 20 ho to behtar hai, lekin sabse zaroori shart ADX ka upward trending hona hai chahe value koi bhi ho.
- Entry: Teeno conditions match hone wali signal candle ke High ke upar agle candle par buy entry li jaati hai.
- Stop-Loss: Entry candle / pichli candle ka Low (previous candle's low) strict stop-loss hota hai.
- Exit Conditions: (1) Stochastic RSI jab overbought territory (80-95 zone) me pahunch kar lines converge ya cross karne lage tab close par exit; (2) Agar trade lene ke baad Stochastic RSI overbought na ho aur wapas oversold me chala jaye to turant exit; (3) Target-based exit: Daily timeframe par 3-6% (7-8 din me), Weekly timeframe par 7-10% (lagbhag 1 mahine me), aur Monthly timeframe par 12-50% (lagbhag 1 saal me).
