## Point-by-point
- Atoms vs Bits Value Migration: World market me capital and earnings physical asset-based companies (Atoms) se digital platform companies (Bits) ki taraf shift ho rahe hain. Bits companies low marginal cost, near zero replication cost, and high network effects ke karan non-linear scale-up karti hain.
- Bits Company Key Success Factors: High growth potential paane ke liye 6 critical filters hone chahiye: 1. Large TAM (Total Addressable Market), 2. Product-Market Fit, 3. Wide Virality/Distribution, 4. Direct/Indirect Network Effects, 5. Positive Unit Economics (positive unit contribution margin), 6. Operational Scalability.
- Bits Financial Accounting Distortion: Bits companies initial years me technology and customer acquisition (intangible assets) create karne ke liye human capital expensing karti hain jo P&L me charge ho kar optical loss dikhati hain, jabki real free cash flow leveler hota hai.
- Bits Valuation Methodology: Loss-making digital companies ko value karne ke liye 3 methods ka average liya jata hai: Discounted Cash Flow (DCF), Price/Sales to Growth (PSG) ratio benchmark with peer haircut, and Last Private Equity funding round valuation.
- PSG Ratio (Price/Sales to Growth): PSG = (Price / Sales) / (Projected Sales CAGR %). Loss-making hyper-growth companies ko P/S ratio ki jagah PSG ratio se compare karke fair value calculate ki jati hai.
- Consistent Wealth Creator Concept: Aise shares jo pichle 5 saal me har ek saal (5 out of 5 years) benchmark Sensex ko outperform karein.
- All-Round Wealth Creator Ranking Matrix: Composite Rank = Rank(Absolute Wealth Created) + Rank(5-Year Price CAGR) + Rank(Consistency / Number of outperforming years).
