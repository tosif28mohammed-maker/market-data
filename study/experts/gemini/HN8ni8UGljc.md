## Point-by-point
- Barbeque Nation ki parent company (United Foodbrands) ke restaurant business model me sabse badi samasya high rental expenses hain kyunki India me Grade-A commercial spaces (malls, high street areas) ki kami hai.
- India me high commercial real estate rent ki wajah se restaurant failure rate kaafi zyada rehata hai.
- Barbeque Nation me daypart utilization ki samasya hai: yeh business mukhya roop se lunch aur dinner par nirbhar hai, jabki breakfast aur evening snacks time me revenue generate nahi hota.
- Restaurant sector me competition badhne se catchment area chhota ho gaya hai (3-5 km radius tak simit), jis-se har outlet ki revenue ceiling cap ho jaati hai aur regional saturation aata hai.
