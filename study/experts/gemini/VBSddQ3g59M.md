## Point-by-point
- FIIs typically start buying in large-cap stocks and banks first before rotating down the market-cap spectrum into mid-caps.
- During broad market rallies, investors should use price strength to trim/exit stocks that suffer from weak operational performance, rather than holding on blindly.
- Financial services and Indian domestic banks remain attractive long-term due to multi-year low NPAs, strong management teams, and healthy economic fundamentals.
- Promoter exits and M&A transactions often reflect strategic needs to scale rapidly for global competition or deleverage balance sheets.
