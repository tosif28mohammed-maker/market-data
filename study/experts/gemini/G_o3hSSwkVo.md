# MTF Swing Trading Strategy: High Momentum Quality Stock Screener (ScanX)

## Point-by-point
- Yeh setup MTF (Margin Trading Facility) ya normal cash swing trading ke liye banaya gaya hai, jisme technical momentum aur fundamental institutional backing dono ko combine kiya jata hai.
- Timeframe: Daily chart (1-Day candles). Holding period: 1 hafta se 10 din ya jab tak exit conditions trigger na ho.
- Technical Screening Filters: (1) Current Price > 20 EMA, (2) 20 EMA > 50 EMA, (3) Price > Supertrend, (4) ADX > 20, (5) ADX > Previous Candle ADX (rising ADX showing accelerating trend), (6) RSI (14) 55 se 70 ke beech (momentum zone, overbought hone se pehle), (7) Aaj ka volume unusual/high volume hona chahiye, (8) 12-day price return > 5% (pichhle 12 din me stock kam se kam 5% chadh chuka ho).
- Fundamental / Institutional Filters: (1) Latest quarter me FII stake increase hua ho, (2) Latest quarter me DII stake increase hua ho, (3) Market Cap >= Rs 5,000 Crore (mid/large cap quality floor).
- Exit Niyam: Position tab tak hold karni hai jab tak dono shartein barkarar rahein: (1) 20 EMA > 50 EMA bani rahe, aur (2) Price Supertrend ke upar bana rahe. Agar Price Supertrend ke neeche close de de ya 20 EMA 50 EMA ke neeche cut kare to exit karein.
