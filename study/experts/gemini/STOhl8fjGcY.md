# Paytm Q2 Earnings Analysis: Turnaround & Valuation Watchpoints
स्रोत: Groww (YouTube) · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=STOhl8fjGcY · Gemini ने पढ़ा 2026-11-25 09:43 UTC

## Point-by-point
- Paytm reported Q2 FY27 revenue growth of 28% YoY to ₹2,648 crore.
- Reported net profit increased significantly to ₹225 crore (vs ₹21 crore in Q2 FY26), primarily due to higher exceptional impairment charges (₹190 crore) in the base quarter Q2 FY26.
- Excluding exceptional items, net profit growth was modest at 9% YoY (₹211 crore to ₹229 crore).
- Financial services revenue grew 49% YoY to ₹912 crore, driven by 8.8 lakh key financial service customers (up 35% YoY).
- Operating metrics showed growth: Merchant Payment Value increased 26% YoY to ₹7.1 lakh crore, subscription-paying merchants reached 1.64 crore (+20% YoY), and monthly transacting users (MTU) reached 8.5 crore.
- Paytm UPI Gross Transaction Value (GTV) grew at 42% YoY vs industry growth of 19% (2.2x industry rate).
- Key Watchpoint 1: Other income accounts for ₹182 crore out of ₹225 crore reported net profit (80.9% of profit), indicating low core operating profit contribution.
- Key Watchpoint 2: Trailing P/E valuation sits high at around 144.8x, suggesting future business turnaround is heavily priced into the current stock price.
