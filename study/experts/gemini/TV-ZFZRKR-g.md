## Point-by-point
- Indian markets are rarely cheap because top company valuations rarely enter classic bear territory; corrections should be viewed as buying opportunities rather than trend reversals.
- Maintain cash liquidity ('keep powder dry') to capitalize on periodic market pullbacks across global assets (US, Korea, Japan, India).
- For mega-cap tech/AI leaders (e.g., Nvidia) facing news-driven sell-offs, regulatory scrutiny, or accounting probes (sharp pullbacks near 40-50% from peaks), avoid trying to time the exact bottom.
- Implement staggered entry for quality compounders: allocate an initial tranche of 25-30% of target capital upon major pullbacks rather than waiting passively on the sidelines.
