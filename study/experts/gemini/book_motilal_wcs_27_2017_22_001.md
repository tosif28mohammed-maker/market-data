## Point-by-point
- Mid-to-Mega Crossover Strategy: Market Cap rank 101 se 250 ke 150 mid-cap stocks me se agle 5 saal me 12-25 stocks (8-16% strike rate) Mega (Top 100) category me enter karte hain, jo average 42% Price CAGR deliver karte hain (vs Sensex 15%).
- Mini-to-Mid Crossover: Mini stocks (>500 market cap rank) me se Mid category (101-250) me aane wale stocks ne 35% average return CAGR diya; inme se top wealth creators ne 46% CAGR diya.
- Standalone Price/Book < 1.0x Valuation Filter: 2017 me P/B < 1x wale shares ne agle 5 saal me 46% Price CAGR aur 99% PAT CAGR deliver kiya.
- Standalone Price/Sales < 1.0x Valuation Filter: P/S < 1x wale shares ne 37% Price CAGR aur 119% PAT CAGR deliver kiya.
- Payback Ratio < 1x Screen: Current Market Cap ko agle 5 saal ke actual profits se divide karne par Payback Ratio < 1x wale shares ne 31% Price CAGR (87% PAT CAGR) aur 1-2x range wale shares ne 34% Price CAGR deliver kiya.
- PEG < 1.0x Strategy: Trailing 12-month P/E ko future 5-year PAT CAGR se divide karne par PEG < 1x wale stocks ne 35% Price CAGR aur 21% PAT CAGR deliver kiya.
- Consistent Wealth Creator Metric: Sensex ke khilaf 5 me se kam se kam 3-5 saal outperform karne wale stocks consistent outperformers bante hain.
- All-round Wealth Creator Ranking Model: Total Score = (Biggest Wealth Rank + Fastest Price CAGR Rank + Consistency Rank). Sabse kam score wale stocks sabse behtareen all-round risk-adjusted wealth creation dikhate hain.
- Wealth Destruction Avoidance: 2017-22 ke dauran Financials sector sabse bada wealth destroyer raha (26% share, INR 3,689 billion lost), follows by Oil & Gas (11%) and Telecom (10%). Top 10 destroyers me se 4 PSUs the (IOCL, Coal India, PNB, ONGC).
