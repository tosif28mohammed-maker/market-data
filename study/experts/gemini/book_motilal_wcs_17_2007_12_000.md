## Point-by-point
- Motilal Oswal ki 17th Wealth Creation Study (2007-2012) me 'Economic Moat' ko lambi avdhi ke wealth creation ka mukhya zariya bataya gaya hai.
- Market Outperformance Filter: Is saal se naya filter joda gaya ki company ka 5-saal ka stock price CAGR benchmark index (BSE Sensex, jo 6% tha) se zyada hona zaroori hai.
- Economic Moat Quantitative Test: 8 saal ke historical data (1995-2002) par check kiya gaya ki kaun si companies ka annual RoE unki respective sector average RoE se kam se kam 6 saal tak upar raha. Aisi companies ko Economic Moat Companies (EMCs) mana gaya.
- Economic Moat Portfolio Performance: EMCs portfolio ne 2002 se 2012 ke beech 25% CAGR return diya (BSE Sensex ke 18% aur Non-EMCs ke 12% CAGR ke muqable).
- Competitive Advantage Period (CAP) Rollover Effect: Deep Moat wali companies premium valuation par hone ke bawajood outperformance karti hain kyunki bazaar har saal unka CAP 1 saal aage rollover kar deta hai jab tak moat salamat rahe.
- High Dividend Payout + High RoE Correlation: High RoE wali companies jinaka dividend payout >40% hota hai, unhe business me re-invest karne ke liye kam external capital ki zaroorat hoti hai aur wo sustained wealth create karti hain.
