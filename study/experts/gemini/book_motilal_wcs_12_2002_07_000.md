## Point-by-point
- 5-saal ke multi-bagger stocks pehchanne ke liye fundamental screening criteria: P/E < 10x, Price/Book < 1x, Price/Sales <= 1x, aur Payback Ratio (Current MCap / 5-saal projected PAT) <= 1x.
- Small Market Cap Bias: Base year me jin companies ka Market Cap Rs 200 crore (Rs 2 Billion) se kam tha, unka 5-saal ka price CAGR 136% raha.
- Young Company Filter: 10 saal se kam umar ki companies ne 106% price CAGR diya kyunki unka PAT CAGR base chhota hone ki wajah se 52% tha.
- Domestic Demand Filter: High export (>50% of sales) wali companies global risks ke karana underperform karti hain (16-35% CAGR), jabki low/zero export (<25% of sales) wali domestic companies ne 42-64% price CAGR diya.
- Low Initial RoE Turnaround: Base period me RoE <5% wali depressed/blind-spot companies ne business expansion par 72% price CAGR diya, jabki already high RoE (>40%) companies ne sirf 17% CAGR diya.
- Privately Owned vs PSU Filter: Private sector companies ne state-owned PSUs ko lagataar outshine kiya (Price CAGR 42% vs 39%, Earnings CAGR 37% vs 27%).
- Hyper-growth Sales & PAT Filter: Sales growth 40-50% wali companies ne 84% price CAGR diya. PAT CAGR >70% wali companies ne 68-70% price CAGR diya.
