# Motilal Oswal 27th Annual Wealth Creation Study (2017-2022): Consistents & Volatiles

## Point-by-point
- Report corporate India ko do mukhya bhaagon mein baant-ta hai: Consistents (Consistent PAT growth dene wali companies) aur Volatiles (unpredictable PAT wali companies).
- Consistent Company ki quantitative definition: 15 saal ke period mein annual PAT 10% se zyada sirf 3 baar se kam gire (10 saal mein max 2 baar), koi bhi ek saal ki PAT giravat 50% se zyada na ho, aur final year PAT pehle year PAT se kam na ho.
- Data se pata chalta hai ki 87% Consistents ne benchmark (Nifty/Sensex) ko beat kiya (avg 17% CAGR vs Nifty 9%), jabki sirf 33% Volatiles hi beat kar paaye (avg 1% CAGR).
- Growth-Return Matrix: Low PAT growth (<20%) wali Consistent companies ne bhi 13% return CAGR diya, jo ki High PAT growth (>=20%) wali Volatile companies (9% CAGR) se behtar tha.
- Consistency Patch Principle: Chahe stock past 10 saal mein Consistent ho ya Volatile (C-C ya V-C), agar agle 3 saal mein woh 'Consistency Patch' (bina kisi bade PAT drop ke continuous growth) mein jaata hai, toh woh 21% se 28% CAGR return deta hai.
- Consistents Entry Strategy: Consistent stocks ko buy tab karo jab unka Current P/E ratio unke 10-Year Median P/E ratio ke neeche ho. Inme Buy-and-Hold strategy kaam karti hai.
- Volatiles Entry & Exit Strategy: Volatiles mein sirf Large-Cap (top 200 market cap rank) mein kaam karo. Entry tab karo jab Current P/B ratio 10-Year Median P/B ratio ke neeche ho ('Despair' phase). Exit tab karo jab P/B median se kafi upar ho jaaye ('Optimism' phase); inme Buy-and-Hold mat karo.
- Lesser-Known Consistents Setup: Un companies ko screen karo jinaki stock listing history < 10 saal hai lekin financial history >= 10 saal hai, 10-saal avg RoE > 15-20%, 10-saal PAT CAGR > 15%, aur Current P/E < 10-Year Median P/E hai.
- PSU Avoidance: 2017-2022 ke dauran top 100 Wealth Creators mein 99% wealth private companies ne banayi, PSUs ka share sirf 0.3% tha. Isliye long portfolios se PSUs ko filter out karna chahiye.

## Niyam
- [new/yes] 15-Year / 10-Year PAT Growth Consistent Stock Screening Rule: Select stocks where over 15 years annual PAT drops >10% max 3 times (or max 2 times in 10 years), no single year PAT drop > 50%, and terminal PAT >= initial PAT. | entry: Screen and buy stocks meeting the 3-point PAT consistency criteria. | exit: Exit if company violates consistency criteria (e.g. PAT drops >50% or faces >10% PAT drop more than allowed limits). | stop: PAT drop > 50% in a single year or consecutive severe earnings degrowth.
- [new/yes] Consistents P/E Below 10-Year Median Entry Strategy: Buy 'Consistent' stocks when current trailing P/E ratio is lower than its 10-year median P/E ratio. | entry: Buy stock when it qualifies as 'Consistent' and current P/E < 10-Year Median P/E. | exit: Long-term compounding buy-and-hold; exit only if earnings consistency breaks or valuation becomes exuberantly stretched. | stop: Fundamental degradation or breakdown of earnings consistency rule.
- [new/yes] Volatile Large-Cap P/B Below 10-Year Median Mean-Reversion Strategy: For top 200 market-cap volatile stocks, buy when current P/B is below 10-year median P/B and exit when P/B expands significantly above median. | entry: Buy top 200 market-cap volatile stock when current P/B < 10-Year Median P/B. | exit: Sell/exit when P/B expands significantly above 10-year median P/B; strictly do not hold long-term. | stop: Solvency risk or severe structural degradation in book value.
- [new/yes] 3-Year Rolling Consistency Patch Outperformance Setup: Buy any stock (Consistent or Volatile) that enters a 3-year period of steady earnings growth without any PAT drop >10%. | entry: Buy stock showing initiation of 3 consecutive years of positive annual PAT growth with zero drops > 10%. | exit: Exit after 3 years or when PAT drops by > 10% in any annual reporting. | stop: Annual PAT drop > 10%.
- [new/yes] Lesser-Known Consistents Screening Rule: Buy stocks with listed history < 10 years but financial history >= 10 years, having 10-year Average RoE >= 15%, 10-year PAT CAGR >= 15%, and Current P/E < 10-Year Median P/E. | entry: Buy screened stocks meeting listing history < 10 yrs, 10-yr Avg RoE >= 15%, 10-yr PAT CAGR >= 15%, and Current P/E < 10-Yr Median P/E. | exit: Exit when Current P/E expands significantly above median P/E or 10-year Avg RoE drops below 15%. | stop: PAT de-growth > 10% in any two consecutive years.
