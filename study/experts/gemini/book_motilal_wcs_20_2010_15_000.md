## Point-by-point
- Mid-to-Mega (MTM) Crossover Concept: Mid-to-Mega refers to stocks transitioning from Mid-Cap rank (Market Cap ranks 101 to 300) to Mega-Cap rank (Top 100 Market Cap) within a 5-year investment horizon.
- Historical MTM Performance: Across 5-year rolling periods between 2000 and 2015, MTM portfolios delivered a median return of 46% CAGR, generating a 28% median annual alpha over the BSE Sensex.
- The 6-Step MQGLP Backtesting Screening Process for Mid-to-Mega Selection: (1) Starting Universe: Top 101-300 market cap rank companies. (2) Quality of Business: Base year RoE >= 20%. (3) Industry Leadership: Must be ranked #1, #2, or #3 in industry revenue, or a key beneficiary of Value Migration / strong sector tailwinds. (4) Growth Momentum: Preceding 2-year PAT CAGR >= 20%. (5) Longevity: Prefer secular business models over cyclicals. (6) Price/Valuation: Base year P/E ratio <= 25x (ideally around 15x-20x average).
- Mega-Cap Stability & Leadership: 88 out of the top 100 Mega companies in India are top 3 industry leaders in their respective segments, and 73 out of 100 maintain a 5-year average RoE > 15%.
- Mega-to-Mid Dropouts Avoidance: 161 cases of companies slipping out of Top 100 ranks were analyzed. Major causes: Cyclical downturns (35%), Management lapses (29%), Reverse Value Migration (12%), and Capital Misallocation (8%). Crucially, 75% of dropouts were non-leaders in their industries.
- Quality Trap Warning: High RoE (> 20%) combined with high valuation but low PAT CAGR (< 10%) leads to severe stock underperformance (e.g., Hero MotoCorp with 51% RoE but 2% PAT CAGR generated negative alpha against Sensex).
- Macro Valuation Bottoming Indicators: Long-term average Corporate Profit to GDP is 5.0%. When Corporate Profit to GDP drops below 4.0% and Market Cap to GDP drops below 76%, it signals a major structural bottom for long-term equity accumulation.
