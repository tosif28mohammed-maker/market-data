# Dhan: Bank Nifty 1-Minute 200 EMA Scalping Strategy

## Point-by-point
- Instrument & Timeframe: Bank Nifty index chart par 1-minute timeframe.
- Option Selection Filter: ATM ya 100 points ITM options chunein (OTM bilkul avoid karein). Delta >= 0.60 hona chahiye (agar ATM me delta 0.55-0.56 ho tabhi ATM lein, warna ITM).
- Core Indicator: 200 Exponential Moving Average (EMA).
- Entry Condition (Long): Price 200 EMA ke touch par ho (ya bilkul paas) aur Bullish Engulfing ya Morning Star pattern bane. Wicks chote hone chahiye aur green candle pichle red bar ka minimum 90% cover karni chahiye.
- Entry Condition (Short): Price 200 EMA ke touch par ho (ya pullback touch) aur Bearish Engulfing ya Evening Star pattern bane.
- Bar Size Filter: Bahut bade bars (oversized candles) ko avoid karein kyunki stop loss bada ho jata hai; normal sized bars chunein jisme stop loss chota rahe.
- Stop Loss: Signal bar ya swing high/low ke bahar. Bank Nifty options me stop loss 40-50 points ke andar hona chahiye (agar option me SL > 50-60 points ho to trade skip karein).
- Target & Trailing: Minimum 1:2 se 1:3 target plan karein. Agar trade 1:1 ya 1:1.5 favour me chala jaye (jaise 20 point SL par 25 points move), to SL cost-to-cost par trail karein.
- Exit: Target hit hone par ya reverse Bullish/Bearish engulfing candle banne par exit karein.
- Trade Frequency: Mahine me lagbhag 15-20 trades milte hain (din me average 1-2 trades).
