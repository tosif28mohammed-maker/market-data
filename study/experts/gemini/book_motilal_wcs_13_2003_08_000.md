## Point-by-point
- Companies ko Return on Equity (RoE) aur Capital Consumption ke aadhar par teen shreniyo me baanta gaya hai: Great, Good, aur Gruesome.
- Adjusted RoE Calculate karne ka formula: Excess cash wahi balance sheet me rehne par PAT se 7% Cash Income ghatiye (Adjusted PAT) aur Net Worth se excess cash ghatiye (Adjusted Net Worth). Adjusted RoE = Adjusted PAT / Adjusted Net Worth.
- Great Companies: 10-saal ka average Adjusted RoE > 25%, pichle 10 saalo me kisi bhi saal Adjusted RoE 15% se kam nahi hona chahiye, aur RoE ka trend badhta hua (rising) hona chahiye. Ye asset-light hoti hain, dividend payout high (70-90%) hota hai aur kam incremental capital par badhti hain.
- Good Companies: 10-saal ka average Adjusted RoE 10% se 25% ke beech, RoE 15-25% zone me, moderate to high capital requirement aur dividend payout 20-30% ke aas-paas hota hai.
- Gruesome Companies: 10-saal ka average Adjusted RoE < 10%. Ye high growth show karti hain par continuous high capital demand karti hain bina profit ya RoE generate kiye (jaise Airlines, Telecom losses). Inhe avoid karna chahiye.
- Bazaar ke Bottom aur 30-40% Rebound ki pehchaan: Jab Trailing Earnings Yield (Sensex 1/PE) aur 10-Year G-Sec Bond Yield ka ratio >= 1.4x ho jaye (historical average 0.73x hai), Sensex trailing P/E historic low ~11x par ho, aur Market Cap to GDP ratio mean (~46-55%) ke paas ho, to bazaar me sharp recovery (30-40%) ki probability bano hoti hai.
- Multi-bagger Valuation Filters (2003-2008 dataset se): Base year me P/E < 10x, Price/Book < 1x, Price/Sales <= 1x, aur Payback ratio <= 1x par available shares ne sabse zyadatar 50%+ Price CAGR deliver kiya.
