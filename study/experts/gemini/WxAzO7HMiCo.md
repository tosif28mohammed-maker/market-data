# NDTV Profit Townhall: Sanjay Mookim on AI Capex Growth, Nifty Target, and Financials sector Strategy
स्रोत: NDTV Profit (YouTube) · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WxAzO7HMiCo · Gemini ने पढ़ा 2026-11-27 14:32 UTC

## Point-by-point
- Sanjay Mookim (Head - India Equity Research, J.P. Morgan) emphasizes separating the business of AI from stock valuation volatility. AI capex (data centers, infrastructure capacity) is projected to grow by 60% to 70% in 2027 over 2026.
- At current token prices, the payback period for commissioning a new AI data center is estimated at 2 to 3 years, making ongoing capex spending economically sustainable.
- J.P. Morgan maintains a December 2027 Nifty target of ~29,000, derived mathematically from an expected 13% to 14% annual EPS growth, assuming market valuation multiples remain stable at current levels.
- Financials is highlighted as the primary sector to drive growth. Financial earnings are directly linked to Nominal GDP growth; even if Real GDP slows, Nominal GDP is expected to stay in double digits for the coming quarters, while Financials sector valuation multiples remain comfortable.
- India's Private Capex upcycle (FY22-26 CAGR at +14%) is driven by multiple catalysts: monetary/tax stimulus, currency depreciation boosting exports, tariff reductions, and the 'World + 1' manufacturing shift.
