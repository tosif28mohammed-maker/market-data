## Point-by-point
- Dhan Option Trader Web ke Insights tab me advanced volatility analysis tools uplabdh hain.
- Platform par India VIX, major indices ka comparative IV, alag-alag expiries aur strikes ka IV track kiya ja sakta hai.
- Volatility ko historical context me dekhne ke liye IV Rank aur IV Percentile ka prayog kiya jata hai.
- Tool me ATM IV ko price ke saath, standard deviation zones, aur high/low volatility stocks dhundhne ke liye IV Mover Screener diya gaya hai.
