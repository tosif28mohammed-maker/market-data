# 3-Indicator Emotionless Swing Trading Strategy (Triple EMA + Stochastic RSI + ADX)
स्रोत: Dhan (YouTube) · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9fq1eBj_9Ok · Gemini ने पढ़ा 2026-11-25 09:43 UTC

## Point-by-point
- Trading philosophy: Trading should be rule-based and boring (math/statistics focused) rather than exciting/emotional. Avoid trade management based on greed, fear, hope, or overconfidence.
- The 3-Indicator Combination for Swing/Positional Trading:
- 1. Trend Indicator: Triple Exponential Moving Averages (EMA) in 1:3:9 ratio. Suggested parameters: 5-15-50 EMA (aggressive) or 9-30-100 EMA (conservative). Must be in bullish alignment (Fast EMA > Medium EMA > Slow EMA).
- 2. Entry Trigger: Stochastic RSI. Must give a BUY crossover rising out of the oversold zone (< 20) while the moving average structure is already bullish.
- 3. Momentum Confirmation: ADX (Average Directional Index). ADX line must be sloping upwards (rising bullish momentum), ideally above 20.
- Entry Rule: Buy on the next candle after a signal candle where all 3 indicator conditions align simultaneously, entering above the signal candle's high.
- Exit Rule: Exit the trade when Stochastic RSI enters the overbought territory (> 80) and its lines start converging or crossing downward.
- Stop-Loss: Low of the previous candle / entry signal candle.
- Expected returns by timeframe:
- - Daily Chart: 3% to 6% per trade over a few days/weeks.
- - Weekly Chart: 7% to 10%+ per trade over weeks/months.
- - Monthly Chart: 12% to 50%+ per trade over several months.
