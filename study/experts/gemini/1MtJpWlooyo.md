## Point-by-point
- Mutual Fund Structure (5 Main Entities): 1. Sponsor (Promoter jo fund shuru karta hai aur license leta hai), 2. Trust/Trustees (Investors ke behalf par assets ki dekhbhal), 3. Fund House/AMC (Front-end operational team, research, sales aur fund management), 4. Custodian (SEBI-regulated bank jo shares/bonds ko Scheme ke naam par rakhta hai, AMC ke naam par nahi), 5. RTA (CAMS/KFintech jo accounting, KYC aur transactions sambhalte hain).
- AMC Bankruptcy/Failure Risk: Agar AMC/Fund House doob jaye to bhi investor ka paisa doobta nahi hai kyunki underlying assets Custodian ke paas Scheme ke naam par hote hain. Aise case mein ya to doosra fund house scheme ko take over karta hai ya scheme likwidaite karke paisa wapas milta hai (jaise 2020 Franklin Templeton debt schemes liquidation jisme 2023 tak principal + 8% total return mila).
- Performance Risk vs Bankruptcy Risk: AMC doobne ka risk nahi ke barabar hai, lekin underlying stocks/bonds ke doobne ya market volatility ka performance risk hamesha rehta hai. Equity markets mein multiple years zero return reh sakta hai.
- Asset Class Risk-Return Hierarchy: Govt Treasury Bonds (safest, lowest return) -> SBI FDs -> Private Bank FDs -> Small Finance Bank FDs -> Corporate Debentures/Bonds (Avoid - high default risk) -> Gold/Real Estate -> Equity Mutual Funds (~12% historical 40-year CAGR in India) -> F&O / Crypto / Forex (High risk / Speculative / Gambling - Avoid).
- NAV Calculation Formula: Net Asset Value (NAV) = (Total Investments Market Value + Cash Held - Total Expenses & Dues) / Total Number of Units. NAV har din market close hone ke baad mark-to-market calculate hoti hai.
- NAV Property on Capital Inflow/Outflow: Naye units issue hone ya redeem hone se NAV par koi asar nahi padta kyunki numerator (cash/investments) aur denominator (units) proportionally adjust hote hain. NAV sirf underlying assets ki performance se badhti/ghatthi hai.
- NFO (New Fund Offer) Myth vs Reality: NFOs ko avoid karna chahiye aur established track record wale funds ko prefer karna chahiye. Low NAV (jaise Rs 10) se zyada ya jaldi return nahi milta; percentage return NAV ki absolute value par nahi balki underlying portfolio ki percentage growth par depend karta hai.
- Fixed Return Guarantee Fraud: Equities ya Mutual Funds mein fixed returns (jaise Telegram/WhatsApp par 2%-4% monthly return) promise karna illegal aur scam hai.
