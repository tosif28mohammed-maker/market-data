## Point-by-point
- Sensex 15-year historical average P/E is 14.3x. When Sensex P/E reaches 20% above this average (~17.1x or higher), further P/E re-rating is limited.
- India Market Cap to GDP ratio reaching near 109% (against a long-term average of 49%) signals peak valuation levels with limited safety margin.
- Earnings Yield to Bond Yield ratio falling below its 15-year average of 0.87x indicates zero margin of safety in the equity market.
- In high market valuation or equity paper supply environments, cash-rich companies with high Return on Capital (RoC/RoE) that fund growth internally without fresh equity capital dilution sustain higher P/E multiples.
