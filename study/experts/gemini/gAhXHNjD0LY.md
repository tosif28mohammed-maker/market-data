## Point-by-point
- Technical analysis classifies stock movements into three main trends: Uptrend, Downtrend, and Sideways.
- Uptrend is characterized by higher highs and higher lows, breaking previous resistance levels to form new highs.
- Downtrend is characterized by lower highs and lower lows, breaking previous support levels to form new lows.
- Sideways trend occurs when high and low points remain within a horizontal price range without forming higher highs or lower lows.
- For long/buy-side positional trading (holding period of a few months), traders should strictly select stocks that are in a clear uptrend.
