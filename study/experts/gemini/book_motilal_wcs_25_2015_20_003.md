## Point-by-point
- Kitaab me 1991-1996 se 2014-2019 tak ke 24 pichle Wealth Creation Studies ka lagbhag har ek theme aur formula samjhaaya gaya hai.
- Study 6 me Enterprise Valuation ke liye exact mathematical formula diya gaya hai: Value = C * (RoC - G) / (R - G), jahan C = Capital Employed, RoC = Return on Capital, G = Growth in Capital Employed, aur R = Cost of Capital.
- Study 2 me RoE ko teen hisso me todne ka tariqa bataya gaya hai (DuPont Analysis): RoE = PAT Margin * Asset Turnover * Gearing (Assets/Equity). Wealth Creators me PAT Margin badhta hai, Asset Turnover stable rehta hai aur Gearing ghati hai.
- Study 10 ke anusar Most Consistent Wealth Creators ke 4 mukhya laksan hote hain: Consumer-facing business, non-cyclical nature, market leadership, aur high Return on Net Worth (RoE).
- Study 16 me High-Quality Blue Chips chunne ke 6 strict quantitative criteria aur 2 buy signals diye gaye hain.
- Study 12 me Macro Trend bataya gaya hai ki har 5-7 saal me India ki GDP me next Trillion Dollar judne par Financial Services, Capital Goods, Cement aur Steel sectors me exponential earnings boom aata hai.
