# Motilal Oswal 23rd Annual Wealth Creation Study (2013-2018): Valuation Insights - What Works, What Doesn't

## Point-by-point
- Intrinsic value is driven by two main factors: Return on Equity (RoE) and Earnings Growth (PAT Growth). Value creation happens only when RoE is strictly higher than the Cost of Equity (assumed at 13% for India: 8% risk-free rate + 5% equity risk premium).
- If RoE equals the Cost of Equity (13%), earnings growth creates zero intrinsic value, and the fair P/E remains constant at 1 / Cost of Equity = 7.7x (approx 8x). If RoE is below 13%, high earnings growth actually destroys firm value.
- For high RoE companies (> 30-40%), P/E flattens out beyond 40-50% RoE. Therefore, high-RoE companies should focus on expanding earnings growth rather than further RoE expansion. Conversely, low-RoE companies (< 15%) gain far more value by increasing RoE (1% higher RoE adds 12% to value) than by increasing growth (1% higher growth adds only 3%).
- Hyper earnings growth (>= 25% PAT CAGR) is rarely sustained beyond 5-6 years. Out of 28 companies with >=25% PAT CAGR during 1998-2003, only 8 sustained it over 2003-2008, and only 1 sustained it over 2008-2013.
- Historical 20-year performance test (1998-2018): High RoE (> 15%) + High PAT Growth (> 15%) stocks delivered 27% average 10-year CAGR vs 14% Sensex CAGR. Low RoE (< 15%) + Low Growth (< 15%) stocks delivered only 6% CAGR (wealth destroyers).
- Pricing Heuristics Backtest Results (1998-2015 3-year/5-year alpha over Sensex): PEG < 0.5x delivered +26% 3-year alpha; PEG 0.5x-1x delivered +11% alpha; PEG > 3x delivered -10% alpha. Payback Ratio < 1x delivered +17% 5-year alpha; Payback Ratio > 3x delivered -12% alpha. P/E < 10x delivered +5% 3-year alpha; P/E > 50x delivered -14% alpha. Relative P/E <= 1x market delivered +4% alpha; Relative P/E > 2x market delivered -8% alpha.
- Methodology to unlock hidden value in cash-rich companies: Deduct excess cash equivalents from Net Worth to get Core Business Net Worth, and deduct 4.5% post-tax cash yield from PAT to get Core Business PAT. Re-rate candidates where Core Business RoE is dramatically higher than reported Balance Sheet RoE.
- Porter's 5-Forces Industry Scoring Model: Rate each of the 5 forces (Rivalry, Customer Power, Supplier Power, Entrants, Substitutes) on 0, 0.5, 1. Select industries with total score >= 3.5 (e.g. Paints 5.0, Cigarettes 5.0, FMCG 4.5, 2-Wheelers 4.0, Ports 4.0).
