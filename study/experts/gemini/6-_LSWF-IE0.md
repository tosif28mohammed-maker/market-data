## Point-by-point
- SpaceX ko US FCC se 10 lakh (1 million) orbital compute satellites deploy karne ki regulatory clearance mili hai jise 'Starmind' kaha ja raha hai.
- Is project ke peeche ka logic Earth par bijli, zameen aur cooling resources ki constraints ko bypass karke space me near-constant solar energy ka upyog karna hai.
- FCC deployment timeline: Nov 2033 tak deployment shuru karni hogi, Nov 2035 tak 10% (1 lakh satellites), Nov 2038 tak 50%, aur 2040 tak full deployment ka target hai.
- Mukhya challenges: Space me convection/conduction nahi hoti isliye heat radiation ke zariye nikalna bahut mushkil aur slow hota hai; radiation se chips ko bachana; orbital debris aur collision risks.
- Satellites ke beech mesh networking ke liye laser communication links ki zaroorat padegi, aur Earth tak high-bandwidth data transmission ek bada challenge bana hua hai.
- Indian space ecosystem aur startups (jaise Pixxel) ke liye space tech me opportunities hain lekin overall project commercial viability aur engineering feasibility ke aage abhi naye daur me hai.
