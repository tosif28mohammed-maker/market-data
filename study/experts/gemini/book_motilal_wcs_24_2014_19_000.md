## Point-by-point
- 5 saal ke dauran BSE ki 3,440 listed companies me se 32% (1,104 companies) ne 70% se 100% value lose ki ya delist ho gayi; iska bada kaaran business downturn ke alawa corporate governance aur sharp practices the.
- Management Integrity Formula: Equity investing me 90% management hota hai, 9% industry aur 1% baaki sab. Integrity compromised hone par stock me 'race to zero' hota hai.
- Double-entry Sharp Practice ka mukhya niyam: 'Credit P&L A/c, Debit Balance Sheet'. Managements munafa badhane ke liye P&L me credit leti hain aur financial kachra (trash) Balance Sheet ke asset side (Debtors ya Fixed Assets) par park karti hain.
- Operating Cash Flow vs PAT filter: Agar company revenue inflate karti hai (bogus sales), to Debtors badhte hain aur Operating Cash Flow (OCF) girta hai. OCF-to-PAT conversion ratio track karna mandatory hai.
- Capex/R&D Expense Capitalization Scam: Jab investors OCF dekhne lage, to companies ne operating expenses aur R&D ko Fixed Capital me capitalize karna shuru kiya (Credit Expense, Debit Fixed Assets). Isse OCF achha dikhta hai par Capex badhne se Free Cash Flow (FCF) heavily negative rehta hai.
- Depreciation deflating: Plant & machinery ki useful life 10 saal se badhakar 15 saal karna ya salvage value 5% se 10% karna sidhe depreciation charge 37% tak ghata deta hai, jisse accounting profit artificially inflate hota hai.
- Off-balance sheet debt: Subsidiaries ke through receivables discount karwana with recourse aur corporate guarantee dena (Contingent liabilities me dikhna) hidden leverage hota hai jo Balance Sheet me nahi aata.
- Lending/Financials me Sharp Practices: 5x se 10x leverage par chalne wale banks/NBFCs me income upfronting (high upfront fee income in lieu of annuity interest), large ticket size exposure, aur large illiquid collaterals (jaise Loan Against Promoter Shares) sabse bade disaster triggers hain.
- Immediate warning signals: Statutory auditor ka achanak resignation, top management (CEO, CFO, CS) ka baar-baar badalna, single-digit tax rate (despite high profit), debtor days > 150-300 din, aur promoters ke shares pledge hona.
