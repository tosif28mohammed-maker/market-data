## Point-by-point
- Video analyses 5 midcap companies post Q2 FY27 results based on business models, financials, future outlook, valuations, and risks.
- Company 1 (Indian Hotels - IHCL): Q2 FY27 Revenue +22% YoY (Rs 2,484 Cr), EBITDA +24% (Rs 706 Cr), PAT +23% (Rs 349 Cr). Domestic RevPAR +25%, Occupancy 82% (+7 pts), ARR Rs 16,902 (+17%). 94% of pipeline rooms are asset-light (management contracts/leases). Raised FY27 revenue guidance from 12-14% to 15-17%. Risks include profit dilution from US/UK/catering subsidiaries (~30% H1 revenue) and high valuation (~58.7x P/E).
- Company 2 (Uno Minda): Q2 FY27 Revenue +38% (Rs 6,655 Cr), EBITDA +33% (Rs 733 Cr), PAT +38% (Rs 420 Cr). Headline revenue includes Rs 260-270 Cr aluminium price pass-through effect (underlying revenue growth ~33%). Key growth drivers: Castings & Alloy wheels (+57%), Switches (+37%), Green mobility (+102%). Project portfolio Rs 5,287 Cr (~27% of FY26 revenue). EBITDA margin guidance ~11% +/- 50 bps. Risks include weak cash conversion (H1 OCF +5% vs Revenue +31%), commodity inflation, and high valuation (59.2x P/E vs sector median 27.2x).
- Company 3 (L&T Finance): Q2 FY27 Revenue +27% (Rs 5,484 Cr), PAT +33% (Rs 975 Cr), Loan book +28% (Rs 1.37 Lakh Cr). Retail loan book is 98.5%. Personal loans +72% YoY, Gold finance 3.4x YoY. Funding cost reduced to 6.95% (-25 bps QoQ), NIM improved to 8.88%. Credit cost 2.46% (-52 bps), RoA 2.60%, RoE 13.56%. Target: 20% loan book CAGR to FY31, Q4 FY27 RoA target 2.8-3.0%, FY31 RoE target 16-18%. Risks: Personal + SME loans (~21% of book) untested across full economic cycle; Rural + Farmer loans (~37% of book) sensitive to monsoon/rainfall; borrowing market yields up 30-50 bps.
- Company 4 (Multi Commodity Exchange - MCX): FY23-26 3-Year Sales CAGR 65%, PAT CAGR 108%, OPM expanded from 28% to 71%. Business relies ~97% on transaction fees. Futures ADT grew from Rs 26,178 Cr (FY22) to Rs 64,407 Cr (FY26). Options Notional ADT grew from Rs 7,860 Cr (FY22) to Rs 4,71,641 Cr (FY26) (~60x). Q2 FY27 Sales +114% (Rs 800 Cr), PAT +131% (Rs 455 Cr), OPM 76%. Valuation ~50x P/E. Risks: De-rating risk if commodity price volatility cools down and trading volume slows; tech & SGF expenses.
- Company 5 (Aditya Birla Capital): Multi-line NBFC + Housing Finance + AMC + Life Insurance. FY23-26 Revenue CAGR ~22%, Adjusted PAT CAGR ~23%. Q2 FY27 Segment Revenue +31% (Rs 16,545 Cr), Consolidated PAT +38% (Rs 1,288 Cr). Total Lending AUM +32% (Rs 2.34 Lakh Cr); NBFC book +26%, Housing Finance book +50%, MSME loan book +30% (57% of NBFC book). Gross Stage-3 loans improved from 1.68% to 1.28%, Credit cost fell to 0.92%. NBFC RoA 2.45%. Target: NBFC RoA ~2.5% by Q4 FY27, 200-300 Gold loan branches by March 2027 (1,000+ in 3 years). Valuation: ~2.7x P/B vs Consolidated RoE ~12%.

- [new/yes] Auto Component Commodity Price Pass-Through Adjustment Screen: When screening manufacturing/auto component companies reporting headline revenue growth > 30%, subtract raw material price pass-through adjustments (e.g., aluminium/steel price indexation) from total revenue to compute true volume-driven revenue growth; only buy if underlying volume-driven growth is > 25% YoY. | entry: Buy stock after confirming underlying volume-driven quarterly revenue growth > 25% YoY (excluding commodity price adjustments) | exit: Exit or avoid if operating cash flow (OCF) growth lags revenue growth by > 20 percentage points due to working capital absorption in receivables/inventory | stop: Exit if EBITDA margin strays > 100 bps below management target guidance corridor
- [new/yes] Financial Exchange Operating Leverage Trigger: Buy exchange/market-infrastructure monopolies/duopolies when transaction fee revenue comprises >= 90% of total revenue, options ADT grows >= 50% YoY, and quarterly operating profit margins (OPM) expand above 60% due to volatility-driven trading volume spikes. | entry: Buy exchange stocks when Options Average Daily Turnover (ADT) grows > 50% YoY and OPM crosses 60% | exit: Exit when commodity/market volatility declines causing Options ADT to stagnate or fall YoY, or when P/E exceeds 50x while turnover growth slows below 20% | stop: Exit on de-rating trigger (turnover growth deceleration)
