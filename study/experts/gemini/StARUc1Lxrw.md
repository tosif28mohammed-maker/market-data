## Point-by-point
- Fed FOMC Policy aur Macro Dynamics: FOMC meeting mein policy rate hold par rakha gaya lekin do hawkish dissents (Hammack aur Cook) dekhe gaye. Central banks debt monetization aur financial repression ke regime mein hain jahan inflation persistent hone ke bawajood aggressive rate hikes implement karna mushkil ho raha hai.
- Global Central Bank Divergence: Single-mandate central banks (jaise ECB aur Bank of Canada) ne energy price spikes ke chalte rate hike kiye, jabki employment aur growth indicators (jaise Canada mein -49.6k payrolls) kamzor ho rahe hain.
- European Energy Crisis: Europe aur Asia mein natural gas ki keematein US Henry Hub benchmark ke muqable lagbhag 11x zyada hain, jisse manufacturing aur industrial base par bhaari dabav ban raha hai.
- AI Capex Cycle aur Hyperscaler Deceleration: Microsoft ($38.37B actual vs $45.93B est) aur Alphabet ($51.36B actual vs $55.78B est) ke capex numbers guidance/estimates se kam rahe. AI infrastructure buildout (data centers, computer equipment investment) jo ab tak GDP growth ka pramukh driver tha, usme capex slowdown aane se AI trade mein exhaustion aur air-pocket risk ban raha hai.
- Credit Markets aur High Leverage Risk: Hyperscalers aur AI infrastructure providers debt/equity issuance par zyada nirbhar ho rahe hain jabki free cash flow margin compress ho rahe hain. Jab commodity ya capex bubble burst hota hai, fixed interest debt obligations meet karne ke liye companies ko excess supply pump karni padti hai, jisse operating margins zero ke paas chale jaate hain.
- Gold aur Liquidity Squeeze: Macro liquidity crunch aur dollar strength ke dauran sovereign entities aur institutions loss cover karne ke liye liquid assets (jaise Gold) bechti hain, jisse gold summer lows ke neeche slip hua.
