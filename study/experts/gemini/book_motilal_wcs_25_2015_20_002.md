## Point-by-point
- CVPM (Cost-Volume-Price-Mix) analysis explains how a 20% volume growth can yield a 60% EBITDA growth due to fixed-cost leverage, price hikes, and favorable mix shifts towards higher-margin products.
- Intrinsic P/E formula is derived as P/E = Payout Ratio / (k - g), where Payout is dividend payout ratio, k is cost of equity, and g is perpetual growth rate.
- Empirical data from 2015-2020 study highlights valuation heuristics performance: P/B < 1x yielded 39% CAGR (highest alpha), P/S < 1x yielded 18% CAGR, P/E < 10x yielded 16% CAGR, while relative P/E > 2x gave -8% negative alpha.
- Mid-to-Mega Crossover setup focuses specifically on stocks ranked 101-250 by market cap that hold top 3 market share in their sector. Over 5 years, ~15 stocks move to Mega (top 100) generating ~20-22% return CAGR.
- Mega Cross-Border M&A Avoidance Rule: 7 major overseas acquisitions by Indian companies (e.g., Tata Steel-Corus, Suzlon-REpower, Indian Hotels-Ritz Carlton) generated negative to muted stock returns (-33% to +12% CAGR over 3 years).
- Large Unpopular Deregulation Setup: Companies with ultra-low price-to-sales (< 0.05x, e.g. HPCL in FY13 with Revenue > Rs 2 Trillion and Market Cap < Rs 100 Billion) generate multibagger returns (~5.5x in 4 years) upon government price deregulation.
- Non-monopoly PSU Exclusion: PSUs accounted for only 3% of wealth created during 2015-20, with 5 out of 7 wealth-creating PSUs belonging strictly to Oil & Gas monopolies.
