## Point-by-point
- Analyzed 112 companies based on quarterly results to identify 5 standout performers using a 6-factor fundamental screener on Screener.in.
- Factor 1: Market Capitalization > Rs 3,000 Crore to ensure liquidity and eliminate small micro-caps.
- Factor 2: YoY Quarterly Sales Growth > Average Sales Growth (indicates revenue acceleration).
- Factor 3: YoY Quarterly Profit Growth > Average Profit Growth (indicates net profit acceleration).
- Factor 4: YoY Quarterly Profit Growth > YoY Quarterly Sales Growth (shows operating leverage and margin expansion).
- Factor 5: Debt to Equity < 1.5 (ensures financial stability and manageable debt).
- Factor 6: CFO / EBITDA > 0 (confirms profit quality and cash conversion).
- The 5 stocks that cleared this 6-factor test were CG Power & Industrial Solutions, Garware Hi-Tech Films, International Gemological Institute, Mankind Pharma, and ITC Hotels.
