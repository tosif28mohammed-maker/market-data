## Point-by-point
- FMCG sector staged its biggest monthly rally in 17 years (November), with Nifty FMCG up 14.5% in a single month.
- Highest monthly gain for FMCG sector since July 2009 after a prolonged underperformance.
- All Nifty FMCG constituents ended in green during the rally, with gains ranging between 8% to 16%. Top gainers included Marico (+16.5%), ITC (+16%), GCPL (+16%), Colgate (+15%), and Emami (+15%).
- Key fundamental drivers for FMCG rally: Median revenue growth of top 60 consumer companies reached 16% in Q2, highest revenue growth in nearly 3 years, and weighted average volume growth picked up to 9% in H1.
- FII/FPI buying returned to FMCG with over $300 million net inflow in the first half of November, though the sector remains underweight in FPI portfolios.
- Valuation re-rating potential: Nifty FMCG current P/E valuation is at 35.4x vs its 5-year average of 43.2x, offering margin of safety.
- Lowest valuation stocks in FMCG pack (FY28E P/E): Bajaj Consumer (18.7x), Emami (21.7x), Jyothy Labs (24.4x).
- Stocks with highest Bloomberg return estimates: Patanjali (24%), Varun Beverages (17%), Britannia (12%).
- Key monitoring factors for FMCG sustainability: Volume growth recovery trajectory, crude oil prices, monsoon deficits, and commodity/input cost inflation.
