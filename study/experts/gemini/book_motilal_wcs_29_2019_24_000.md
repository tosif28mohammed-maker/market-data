# Motilal Oswal 29th Annual Wealth Creation Study (2019-2024): Creating Wealth Through Bruised Blue Chips

## Point-by-point
- Blue Chip Ki Pehchan Criteria: (1) Minimum 10 saal ki listing track record, (2) Top 50 market cap ranking OR (3) Top 250 market cap ranking ke andar ho aur 10-saal ka average Return on Equity (RoE) >= 20% ho.
- Bruised Blue Chip Ki Paribhasha: Aisa Blue Chip share jo apne pichle 5-saal ke peak/high price se 50% ya usse zyada gir chuka ho (drawdown >= 50%).
- Valuation Multiplier Trigger: Bruised Blue Chips ke low level par median Price-to-Book (P/B) ratio ~0.8x se 1.5x tak gir jata hai (aur median P/E ~8x ho jata hai). Inhe tab kharidein jab valuation attractive ho, yani Price/Book ratio <= 2.0x ho.
- Turnaround Catalysts (Healing Triggers): (1) External: Sector tailwind, economic recovery, competitive landscape me sudhar ya favorable regulation. (2) Internal: Management change/leadership shift, non-core/loss-making international subsidiaries ka divestment, debt reduction/restructuring, aur cost rationalization.
- Technical Reversal Patterns: Fundamental timing ke saath-saath bottom entry confirm karne ke liye 4 price patterns par dhyan dein - Double Bottom, Reverse Head and Shoulders, Rounding Bottom, aur Flat Base breakout.
- Capital Allocation 18% RoE Rule (M&M Case Study Model): Business units aur subsidiaries ko evaluate karke strictly target rakhein ki 3-5 saal me minimum 18% RoE achieve hona chahiye; jo subsidiaries is benchmark ko meet nahi kar sakti unhe divest ya exit kar dein.
