# Gold & Silver Crash Factors & Macro Drivers Analysis

## Point-by-point
- Gold fell up to ~33% and Silver fell ~60% from peak, dropping nearly 20% in just 7 sessions.
- MCX December Gold Futures fell -3.84% to Rs 1,22,561/10g and Silver Futures fell -5.99% to Rs 1,48,050/kg in a single session.
- Three primary factors driving precious metal corrections: (1) Hawkish US Federal Reserve comments raising rate hike expectations, pushing US Treasury Yields higher and making non-yielding gold less attractive relative to bonds; (2) Stronger US Dollar making USD-denominated metals more expensive for foreign buyers, dampening global demand; (3) Global tech stocks crash causing investors to sell precious metals to cover equity losses.
- Silver fell more than Gold because Silver is both a safe-haven asset and an industrial metal; demand fear from electronics/AI infrastructure slowdown and China's weak growth puts additional downward pressure on Silver.
- Key macro indicators to track future direction of precious metals: (1) US Fed Interest Rate Signals, (2) US Treasury Yields & US Dollar Index Movement, (3) Global Geopolitical Tensions.
