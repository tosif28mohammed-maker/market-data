## Point-by-point
- Banking Sector Growth Opportunity: Decadal cumulative savings expected to reach $10 trillion by 2020 (rising from 34% to 40% GDS/GDP), driving 5x business growth and 6x profit growth for the banking sector.
- Unusual Judgement Banking Strategy: Allocate into a pair of banking stocks—one small/medium high-growth bank growing at >= 2x industry rate, and one large unpopular bank with top-10 asset size trading at low MCap/Assets.
- Banking Data Screening Factors: Banks categorized by 5-year average asset size, 10-year market share gainers/losers, 5-year average Return on Assets (RoA), and P/B valuation multiples.
- Market Outlook Valuation Triggers: Market downside is limited by earnings growth, but upside is capped when Market Cap to GDP is high (~100%), Sensex 1-year forward P/E is above its 15-year average (14.5x), and earnings yield to bond yield is below its long-period average (0.89x).
