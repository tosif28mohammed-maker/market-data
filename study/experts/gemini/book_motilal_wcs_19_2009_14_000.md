## Point-by-point
- Kitaab me 100-bagger (100x return) shares ko dhoondhne aur hold karne ke liye SQGLP framework (Size, Quality, Growth, Longevity, Price) diya gaya hai.
- Indian stock market me 100x pahunchne ka ausat samay 12 saal hota hai (47% price CAGR). Ek baar 100x idea milne par pehle 1-5 saal ki tezi chhootne par bhi agle saalon me khareedne ka moka milta hai (multi-year window of opportunity).
- Size (S): 100x ke liye company chhoti honi chahiye. Market cap USD 0.5 billion (INR 30 billion / Rs 3,000 crore) se kam hona chahiye. Low institutional holding, low analyst coverage, aur kam volume filters use hote hain.
- Quality (Q): Business me bada Profit Pool hona chahiye, Value Migration (Global ya Local) ka faida mil raha ho, ya unique Niche model/Dominant market share ho. Management me High Integrity, Competence (High RoE/RoCE) aur Growth Mindset hona zaroori hai.
- Growth (G): Earnings growth main engine hai, jo Volume growth, Realization/Price growth, Operating leverage aur Financial leverage ka multiplicative combination hai.
- Longevity (L): High RoE/RoCE ko lambe samay tak sustain karna (Competitive Advantage Period - CAP ko badhana) aur earnings growth ka mean reversion (GDP growth rate par girna) rokna zaroori hai.
- Price (P): Buy price par single-digit P/E (ausat 6x P/E historical 100x stocks me) ya TTM P/E <= 25x hona chahiye taaki earnings growth ke saath-saath valuation P/E re-rating ka 4x-5x booster mile.
- Quality-Growth Matrix: Quality Traps (High Quality + Low Growth) aur Growth Traps (Low Quality + High Growth) se bacho; sirf Enduring Multi-baggers (High Quality + High Growth) chuno.
- Macro Indicators: Sensex Earnings Yield / 10-Year G-Sec Yield ka long-period average 0.85x hai. Market Cap / GDP ratio 77% par hone par 2 saal me GDP parity (~100%) tak ~50% market upside target milta hai.
