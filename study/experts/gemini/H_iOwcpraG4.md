## Point-by-point
- FII aur DII dono ki institutional holding badhne wale stocks ko filter karne ke liye 3 specific screening criteria bataye gaye hain: Change in FII Holding > 1%, Change in DII Holding > 1%, aur Market Capitalization > Rs 3,000 Crore.
- Sirf FII aur DII holding badhna buy signal nahi hai; shortlist hone ke baad company ke revenue/PAT growth, debt status, operating cash flows aur valuations check karna zaroori hai.
