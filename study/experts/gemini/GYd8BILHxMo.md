## Point-by-point
- IPO ke turant baad khareedari karne se bachein kyunki listing ke waqt kaafi utaar-chadaav (volatility) hota hai.
- Bade aur purane investors IPO ko apna profit book karke nikalne ke liye istemaal karte hain, jisse listing ke baad price niche girne ka risk rehta hai.
- IPO aane ke baad kuch hafton (few weeks) tak intezaar karein taaki sthiti sthir ho sake aur market share ki sahi keemat (price discovery) tay kar sake.
