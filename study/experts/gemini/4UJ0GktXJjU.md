## Point-by-point
- Video mein 15-minute timeframe (Nifty 50/stocks) par 3 intraday indicators (EMA Crossover, Supertrend, RSI) ke entry signals aur practical filters bataye gaye hain.
- Indicator 1: 9 EMA aur 21 EMA ka Crossover. 9 EMA 21 EMA ke upar cross kare to Bullish/Buy signal, 9 EMA 21 EMA ke neeche cross kare to Bearish/Sell signal.
- EMA Filter: Sideways market mein EMA signals fail hote hain. Agar crossover ke baad bhi 9 EMA aur 21 EMA ke beech ka gap chhota/narrow rehta hai aur widening nahi hoti, to trade avoid karna hai.
- Indicator 2: Supertrend indicator. Green line bullish trend/demand darshati hai, Red line bearish trend/supply darshati hai.
- Supertrend Filter: Agar Supertrend ki line bilkul horizontal/straight (flat) hai aur slant/slope nahi ho rahi, to signal ignore karna hai kyunki market sideways hai.
- Indicator 3: RSI (Relative Strength Index). Buy Signal tab banta hai jab RSI 30 ke neeche (oversold) jaakar wapas 30/40 ke upar cross kare.
- RSI Sell Signal: Stock ke 70 ya 80 (overbought) zone mein jaane par turant sell nahi karna; jab RSI 70/80 se neeche lautna shuru kare tab sell/profit booking position banani hai.
- RSI Regime Filter: RSI ko SIRF sideways market mein trading ke liye use karein. Strong trending market (higher highs/higher lows) mein RSI overbought sell signals ko avoid karein.
