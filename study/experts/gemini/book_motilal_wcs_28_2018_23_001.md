## Point-by-point
- Motilal Oswal ki 28th Wealth Creation Study (2018-2023) 5-saal ke period par Indian stock market me wealth creation, speed, consistency aur market cap rank crossovers ka vishleshon karti hai.
- PEG < 1.0x Filter (Trailing 12M P/E ko 5-Year PAT CAGR se divide karke) sabse solid performance formula raha, jisne 29% price CAGR deliver kiya (benchmark overall 22% ke mukable).
- Small for Speed Rule: Top 10 Fastest Wealth Creators me se 6 companies ka base year (2018) me Market Cap INR 50 Billion (Rs 5,000 Crore) se kam tha.
- Mid-to-Mega Crossover Strategy: Market Cap rank 101 se 250 ke 150 mid-cap stocks me se 12-25 stocks (8-16% strike rate) agle 5 saal me Top 100 Mega category me enter karte hain aur 31% average price CAGR dete hain.
- 5-Year Outperformance Consistency: 100 me se sirf 4 companies hi consecutive 5 saal tak har saal BSE Sensex ko beat kar paayein (Capri Global, Varun Beverages, Grindwell Norton, ICICI Bank).
