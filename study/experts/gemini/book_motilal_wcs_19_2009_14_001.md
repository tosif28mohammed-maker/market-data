## Point-by-point
- PSU vs Private Sector Performance: PSUs accounted for only 2% of total wealth created during 2009-2014 (down from 51% in 2000-05). Only 5 PSUs made it to the top 100 wealth creators.
- PSU Selection Criteria: The 5 PSUs that created wealth matched private sector benchmarks: 5-year Sales CAGR of 17% (vs 18% private), 5-year PAT CAGR of 24% (same as private), and exit RoE of 18% (vs 19% private).
- Market Cap Size Tier Speed: Small-caps in the INR 1-25 Billion (Rs 100-2,500 Cr) range generated the highest Price CAGR of 51%, compared to 37% for INR 25-50 Billion, 40% for INR 50-100 Billion, and 30% for >INR 100 Billion.
- Age Factor: 52 out of top 100 wealth creators were <= 30 years old. Younger companies (age 1-15 years) delivered 41% Price CAGR on 28% PAT CAGR.
- Earnings CAGR Tier Impact: PAT CAGR <10% gave 25% Price CAGR; 10-20% PAT CAGR gave 28%; 20-30% PAT CAGR gave 38%; 30-40% PAT CAGR gave 42%; >40% PAT CAGR gave 46% Price CAGR.
- High RoE vs High Growth Trap: High base RoE (>35%) with low growth (14% PAT CAGR) yielded below-average Price CAGR (29%). Low base RoE (<15%) with high earnings growth (47% PAT CAGR) generated 35%-37% Price CAGR.
- Wealth Destruction Sectors: Total wealth destroyed was INR 4,185 Billion. Top wealth destroying sectors were Utilities (21%), Capital Goods (11%), Construction/Real Estate (8%), Metals/Mining (8%), and Telecom (7%).
- Top Individual Wealth Destroyers: MMTC (INR 654b destroyed), NTPC (INR 497b), BHEL (INR 255b), JP Power (INR 126b), Reliance Power (INR 121b), SAIL (INR 103b).
