# Bank Nifty 1-Min 200 EMA Scalping Strategy with Engulfing and Star Candlestick Patterns
स्रोत: Dhan (YouTube) · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BjdeVTDnEhc · Gemini ने पढ़ा 2026-11-25 09:43 UTC

## Point-by-point
- Mukul Choudhary outlines a 1-minute intraday scalping strategy specifically optimized for Bank Nifty options trading (ATM or 100-point ITM options with Delta >= 0.55-0.60).
- Chart Setup: 1-minute timeframe on Bank Nifty index chart with a 200-period Exponential Moving Average (EMA).
- Core Trend Filter: Long trades only when price is above 200 EMA; Short trades only when price is below 200 EMA.
- Touch/Pullback Requirement: Entry must happen strictly on or near a touch/retest of the 200 EMA. Do not enter if price is extended away from 200 EMA.
- Long Entry Signal: Formation of a Bullish Engulfing or Morning Star pattern at/near the 200 EMA touch within 1-2 candles. Engulfing body should cover >= 90% of the preceding red candle with small wicks.
- Short Entry Signal: Formation of a Bearish Engulfing or Evening Star pattern at/near the 200 EMA touch within 1-2 candles. Engulfing body should cover >= 90% of the preceding green candle.
- Candle Size Filter: Avoid trades if the pattern candle is excessively large (which expands stop-loss distance).
- Stop-Loss: Placed below the pattern low for Longs, or above the pattern high for Shorts (typically 40-50 points in Bank Nifty option terms).
- Target & Trailing: Initial risk-reward target of 1:2 to 1:3. Once trade moves 1:1 to 1:1.5 in profit (e.g., +25 points on a 20-point SL), move stop-loss to breakeven (cost-to-cost). Trail until an opposing candlestick pattern forms.
