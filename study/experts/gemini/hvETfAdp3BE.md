# NDTV Profit Know Your Company (KYC) Analysis of IMFA: Fundamental Screen for Integrated Ferro Chrome Producer

## Point-by-point
- NDTV Profit's 'Know Your Company' (KYC) show evaluated Indian Metals & Ferro Alloys Ltd (IMFA), India's largest value-added ferro chrome producer.
- Key competitive advantage highlighted is full backward integration: captive chromite mining (0.8 mn TPA expanding to 1.2 mn TPA) and captive power generation (205 MW thermal + 135 MW hybrid renewable tie-up), eliminating raw material supply risks.
- Financial profile analyzed: Trailing/FY26 Forward P/E of ~7.4x, EV/EBITDA of ~5.5x, RoCE of 14-16%, RoE of 16.8-17%, and Net Debt Free balance sheet.
- Expansion Capex: Board approved ₹375 Crore capex for chrome ore mining expansion and low-carbon ferro chrome capacity, raising total ferro chrome capacity to 5.34 lakh TPA.
- Revenue Mix: Exports account for ~60-84% of total sales, with domestic sales making up ~15-40% depending on domestic stainless steel demand.
- Management Outlook (Subhrakant Panda, MD): FY27 ferro chrome production guided at 380,000 MT, FY28 guidance at 475,000 MT; company aims to be completely debt-free (long-term debt paid off) by end of 2028.
