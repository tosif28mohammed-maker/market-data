## Point-by-point
- Private Entrepreneur stocks increased their share of total Indian market capitalization from 47% in 1999 to 73% in 2019, while PSU share fell from 21% to 14% and MNC share fell from 32% to 14%.
- Mid-to-Mega crossovers (stocks ranked 101-300 by market cap moving to top 100) generated 34-37% average return CAGR over 5 years compared to 12% benchmark returns.
- Payback Ratio < 1x (Market Cap divided by cumulative 5-year PAT) delivered the highest price CAGR of 36% among all valuation brackets.
- PEG ratio (Trailing P/E divided by 5-year PAT CAGR) < 0.5x delivered extraordinary returns with 52% average price CAGR.
- Wealth destruction was concentrated heavily in Telecom (17%), Public Sector Banks / Financials (17%), Metals/Mining (10%), and Cyclical Autos/Oil & Gas.
