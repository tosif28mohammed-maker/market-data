## Point-by-point
- Economic Profit (EP) is defined as Accounting Profit (AP) minus Equity Charge (Net Worth * Cost of Equity 10%), which simplifies to EP = Net Worth * (RoE - 10%). EP is superior to AP because AP ignores the cost of shareholder equity employed.
- Over 2014-2023, an equal-weighted portfolio of top 500 stocks with positive EP generated an average annual return of 24% with an 8% Alpha over Nifty 500 (outperforming in 8 out of 10 years). In contrast, Economic Loss (EL) stocks delivered only 17% average return (2% Alpha).
- Top 500 stocks by market cap can be mapped into 5 EP Power Curve Quintiles: Quintiles 1 to 3 have positive EP, while Quintiles 4 and 5 represent Economic Loss.
- Hockey-Stick Returns (HSR) are defined as achieving >= 25% compounded price CAGR over 10 years. 84% of HSR upmovers originate from EP Quintiles 2 and 3, or move into Quintiles 1 and 2.
- HSR probability is significantly higher in small/mid-caps at purchase: Stocks in Market Cap Quintiles 3, 4, and 5 (Market Cap Rank 201 to 500) within EP Quintiles 2 and 3 offer a 21% probability of delivering Hockey-Stick returns vs 5% probability for top 200 market cap stocks.
- Valuation Sweet-Spot for Hockey-Stick Returns: 70% of HSR stocks had a starting trailing P/E <= 20x (median 12x). Stocks bought at P/E <= 20x delivered 35-36% 10-year price CAGR vs 28% for stocks bought at P/E > 20x.
- EP Valuation Metric (PEP-G): PEP (Price to EP = Market Cap / EP) divided by Future EP CAGR. Buying stocks with PEP-G <= 2.0x delivers maximum 10-year compounding (33% CAGR for PEP-G <= 1.0x; 27% CAGR for PEP-G 1.0x-2.0x).
- Dual Drivers of Hockey-Stick Returns: Maximum 10-year price CAGR (38%) occurs when a stock achieves 10-year EP CAGR >= 15% AND experiences a 2x P/E expansion (Quadrant 4 setup).
