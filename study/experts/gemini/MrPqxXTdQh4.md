## Point-by-point
- Kunal Shah (Citi) highlights private banks as the preferred sub-sector in financials during a rising interest rate environment due to margin tailwinds (NIM expansion) and defendability against asset quality risks.
- Large-cap banking picks: ICICI Bank and Axis Bank, driven by 20-30% core advance growth in Q2.
- Mid-cap banking pick: RBL Bank.
- Auto sector commentary (Arvind, Citi): 1H growth was strong (~20%) due to GST/low base; 2H growth expected to moderate to high single-digits for PVs and 2Ws with manageable inventory levels (retail matching wholesale). Tractors face slower growth due to adverse weather.
