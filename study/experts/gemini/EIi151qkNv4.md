# PR Sundar Market Analysis & Options Trading Strategies

## Point-by-point
- Macro Decadal Crash Cycle: In Indian markets, major crashes recur roughly every 8 to 12 years (1991 Harshad Mehta scam, 2000 Dot-com crash, 2008 Lehman collapse, 2020 COVID crash). Following multi-year consolidation phases, markets typically experience a 2-3 year strong bull run prior to the next decadal crash.
- Bull Market Sector Rotation: In a structural bull market, capital continuously rotates from overextended/overbought sectors to lagging sectors. When a sector surges sharply (e.g., Bank Nifty +20% in a month), avoid chasing it. Look for contrarian opportunities in lagging sectors showing early positive momentum (e.g., FMCG stocks like Nestle and Marico).
- Asymmetric Options Strangle & Expiry Roll-Over Strategy: In a bullish short-term setup (driven by strong FII inflows and positive GIFT Nifty signals), sell Put options closer to the spot price and Call options further OTM. If Nifty closes below the sold Put strike on expiry day, book the loss and roll over by re-selling the same Put strike for the next week's expiry series.
- Multi-Timeframe Options Hedging: Combine short-term (weekly) option selling with longer-term (1-2 months out) positional trades. If the market dips, short-term Put losses are managed via weekly roll-overs while positional trades move into higher profit comfort zones, balancing overall portfolio MTM.
