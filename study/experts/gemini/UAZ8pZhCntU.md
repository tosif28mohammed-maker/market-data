## Point-by-point
- SEBI 2017 Reclassification: SEBI ne mutual funds ke naam aur categorisation ko standardize kiya taaki AMCs ek hi category mein multiple funds launch karke investors ko confuse na karein.
- Bazaar mein Market Cap Calculation = Total number of listed shares * Price of single share.
- SEBI Market Cap Tiers: Rank 1 se 100 tak = Large Cap; Rank 101 se 250 tak = Mid Cap; Rank 251 se aage = Small Cap.
- Large Cap Fund Mandate: Total AUM ka kam se kam 80% paisa Top 100 Large Cap companies mein invest karna anivarya hai. Baki 20% cash ya anya market cap mein rakh sakte hain.
- Mid Cap Fund Mandate: Total AUM ka kam se kam 65% hissa Rank 101 se 250 wali Mid Cap companies mein invest hona zaroori hai.
- Small Cap Fund Mandate: Total AUM ka minimum 65% hissa Rank 251 aur uske neeche wali Small Cap companies mein invest karna mandatory hai.
- Multi Cap Fund Mandate: Minimum 25% Large Cap mein, 25% Mid Cap mein aur 25% Small Cap mein lagana anivarya hai (kul 75% equity allocation mandatory, baki 25% flexible).
- Flexi Cap Fund Mandate: Kul AUM ka minimum 65% equity mein hona chahiye; fund manager ko poori aazadi hoti hai ki woh Large, Mid ya Small cap mein apni marzi se allocation badal sake.
- Focused Funds: SEBI mandate ke anusar portfolio mein maximum 30 stocks hi hold kiye ja sakte hain. Speaker ke anusar 25-30 stocks par portfolio diversification ke benefits peak ho jate hain, uske baad extra stocks add karne se risk-reduction benefit nahi milta.
- ELSS (Equity Linked Savings Scheme): 3-saal ka mandatory lock-in period hota hai. New tax regime mein 80C ka tax benefit na milne par bhi disciplined investing ke liye upyogi hai.
- Sectoral / Thematic Funds Avoidance: Narrow mandate (jaise Pharma, IT, AI, EV) wale thematic funds ko speaker ne avoid karne ki salah di, kyunki sectoral downturn mein severe drawdown hota hai aur timing galat hone par lambe samay tak capital phans sakti hai.
