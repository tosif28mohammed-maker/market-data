# Effect of Demonetisation on Indian High Denomination Currencies on Indian Stock Market and its Relationship with Foreign Exchange Rate

**काम का नहीं:** Yeh paper ek academic econometrics study hai jo GARCH(1,1) model ka upyog karke Demonetisation period (Nov-Dec 2016) ke dauran Nifty 50 aur USD/INR ke daily returns aur volatility ka statistical analysis karta hai. Isme koi concrete trading strategy, setup, entry/exit point, stop-loss, ya target level nahi diya gaya hai jo automated backtesting ya trading me seedhe kaam aa sake.

## Point-by-point
- Paper 1 April 2016 se 31 March 2017 tak ke 243 daily trading days ke Nifty 50 aur USD/INR exchange rate returns ka adhyayan karta hai.
- OLS Regression model ke anusar, Nifty daily return aur USD/INR daily return me strong negative relationship (coefficient -0.8937, p-value < 0.01) paya gaya, yaani Jab USD/INR ki kimat badhti hai (Rupee kamzor hota hai) to Nifty me giravat ki prabritti dekhi gayi.
- Demonetisation period (9 Nov 2016 se 31 Dec 2016) ko GARCH (1,1) model me Dummy Variable (Dummy = 1) ke roop me measure kiya gaya, jisse pata chalta hai ki is dauran market return volatility me temporary badhotari (clustering effect) hui thi.
- GARCH(1,1) volatility modeling me Generalised Error Distribution (GED) sabse achha model paaya gaya jiska Akaike Information Criterion (AIC) sabse kam (2.1775) tha.
