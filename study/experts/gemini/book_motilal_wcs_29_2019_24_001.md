## Point-by-point
- 2019-2024 ke dauraan top 100 Wealth Creators ne INR 138 trillion ki wealth create ki, jo ki 26% CAGR ki raftaar se Sensex ke 14% CAGR ke muqable kafi aage rahi.
- Top 10 companies ka share total wealth creation me 37% raha, jo pichle 20 saalo me sabse kam hai. Isse jahir hota hai ki wealth creation mid-cap aur small-cap me vyapak (widespread) rahi.
- Turnaround / Loss-making companies ne highest returns diye: Base year 2019 me loss-making stocks ne agle 5 saalo me 46% Price CAGR ka return diya. Negative Net Worth wale stocks ne 38% Price CAGR diya.
- Market Cap Rank Crossover Analysis (2019-2024):
- Mid-to-Mega (Rank 101-250): 150 Mid-cap stocks me se 23 stocks (15.3%) Mega category (Top 100) me pahuche aur unhone average 40% Price CAGR return diya. 70 stocks Mid me hi rahe (21% CAGR) aur 57 stocks Mini me gir gaye (2% CAGR).
- Mini-to-Mid (Rank 251+): 2,852 Mini stocks me se sirf 27 stocks Mid category me pahuche (average 52% CAGR).
- Mini-to-Mega: 2,852 Mini stocks me se sirf 1 stock (Adani Green) Mega category me pahucha (118% CAGR).
- Mega-to-Mega (Top 100): 99 Mega stocks me se 70 Mega me bane rahe (17% CAGR), 26 Mid me gire (5% CAGR), aur 3 Mini me gire (-22% CAGR).
- Valuation parameters multi-bagger performance:
- Payback Ratio < 1x wale stocks ne 37% Price CAGR aur 48% PAT CAGR deliver kiya.
- PEG < 1.0x (P/E / 5-year PAT CAGR) wale stocks ne 35% Price CAGR aur 23% PAT CAGR deliver kiya.
- Price/Sales < 1.0x wale stocks ne 36% Price CAGR deliver kiya.
- Price/Book < 1.0x wale stocks ne 33% Price CAGR deliver kiya.
- Wealth Destruction Analysis: Total Wealth Destroyed INR 4.3 trillion raha (Wealth Created ka 3%). Total wealth destroyed me se 45% hissa (INR 1,912 billion) Financials sector se aaya. Top 10 Wealth Destroyers me se 5 Financials ke the (Bandhan Bank -19% CAGR, Yes Bank -39%, Sammaan Capital -26%, RBL Bank -19%, Dhani Services -35%).
- PSU Performance: 20 PSUs ne total wealth creation ka 17% contribute kiya. PSU Financials ki PAT 5 saal me 19x badhi aur Coal India ki PAT 4x badhi. PSUs ka PAT CAGR 29% raha (Private sector 20% ke muqable) jabki Price CAGR dono ka 25% raha.
- Consistent Wealth Creation is rare: 100 me se sirf 6 stocks (Linde India, Varun Beverages, HAL, BEL, Thermax, NMDC) ne pichle sabhi 5 saalo me har saal Nifty TRI ko outperform kiya.

## Niyam
- [new/yes] Loss-to-Profit (L-to-P) Turnaround Screening Setup: Un loss-making ya negative net worth wali companies me long-term position lo jo balance sheet restructuring, debt repayment, aur capacity ramp-up ke zariye Loss-to-Profit (L to P) turnaround deliver karti hain. | entry: Base year me loss-making (Negative P/E) ya Negative Net Worth wali company me entry lo jab operational turnaround signal (debt cut > 50%, capacity utilization ramp-up) dikhai de. | exit: 5 saal ke holding period par rebalance karo ya jab P/E re-rating fully price in ho jaye. | stop: Operational cash flow negative bane rehne par ya debt fir se peak levels par pahunchne par exit.
- [new/yes] Financials Sector Stressed Asset Quality Avoidance / Short Rule: Financials (Banks/NBFCs) me high credit cost, high NPA, aur asset quality deterioration wale stocks ko portfolio se bilkul avoid karo ya short focus list me rakho. | entry: Financials sector ke un stocks ko portfolio screening me hard-filter karke reject karo jinme Gross NPA aur credit stress badh raha ho (Financials accounts for 45% of total wealth destroyed). | exit: Gross NPA < 2% aur RoA > 1.5% restore hone par filter se hatao. | stop: Asset quality ratios me continuous 2 quarters tak improvement aane par.
- [known/yes] Mid-to-Mega Market Cap Crossover Strategy (Market Cap Rank 101-250 Focus) | entry: Market Cap rank 101 se 250 ke beech ke 150 mid-cap stocks me se high PAT growth prospective stocks select karo. | exit: 5 saal baad rebalance karo. | stop: Market Cap rank 250 se neeche slip hone par position exit karo.
