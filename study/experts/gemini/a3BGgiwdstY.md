## Point-by-point
- Gold experienced a 30% correction due to three main factors: sticky US inflation keeping US Fed interest rate hike expectations high, moderation in trade tariffs/trade deals lowering safe-haven demand, and falling crude oil prices post-Iran conflict lowering inflation-hedge demand.
- Structural support for Gold remains intact due to continuous Central Bank gold accumulation globally.
- Institutional fund strategy (e.g., DSP Multi Asset Omni Fund): Instead of predicting the absolute bottom, systematically accumulate gold at pre-defined price decline levels during corrections.
- Key macro triggers required for a Gold price recovery: Rapid increase in US inflation, slowing/pausing of Fed rate hikes, escalation in geopolitical tensions, or acceleration in central bank gold purchases.
- Three primary parameters to monitor for gold positioning: US interest-rate expectations, US Dollar Index movement, and Central Bank gold purchase volumes.
