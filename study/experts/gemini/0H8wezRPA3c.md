## Point-by-point
- Bharat ka Aerospace Industry do hisson me bata hai: Civil Aerospace aur Defence Aerospace. Defence deals me 25% se 35% tak ka offset clause hota hai, jiske tehat videshi companies ko Bharat me reinvestment / component sourcing karni padti hai.
- Global commercial aerospace me Boeing aur Airbus dominant players hain (combined annual revenue ~$160-170 Billion), aur unke global component sourcing me India ki hissedari abhi sirf 1% se 2% hai.
- Global aircraft order backlog lagbhag 15,000 aircrafts ka hai, aur delivery timeline 2035 ke baad tak extended hai. Global aviation capacity utilization ~85% se upar pahunchne par expansion ki zaroorat padti hai.
- Aerospace supply chain me Tier-2 aur Tier-3 suppliers precision engineering components (jaise airfoils, turbine blades) banate hain. OEM qualification cycle 36 se 48 mahine ka hota hai, jisse high entry barrier aur stickiness banti hai.
- Azad Engineering Case Study: Company gas turbine aur aircraft engines ke cold section aur hot section airfoils/blades banati hai (1,400+ qualified components). Management spending share 1% se badhkar 2-5% hone ki ummeed hai. Company ne 350 kg thrust category ka swadeshi turbojet engine bhi viksit kiya hai.
- Aerospace sector me high inventory aur extended working capital requirement hoti hai kyunki products ki testing aur approval cycle lambi hoti hai.
