## Point-by-point
- FII inflows tend to be exaggerated in MSCI review/rebalancing months (November, February, May, and August) when index weightage revisions trigger forced passive buying.
- Mean reversion is a core financial principle: when Nifty Smallcap 250 outperforms Nifty 50 by around 17% to 18%, a relative mean reversion occurs where Large Caps outperform Smallcaps for the subsequent 1 to 2 months.
- Derivatives market skew (e.g., 27,000 call implied volatility higher than 26,000 call) indicates active domestic short covering in progress.
- Sectoral Focus: Industrials & Capital Goods are strong due to private capex revival, China+1, and defense indigenization. Financial Services/Banking (~30% of Nifty) remain structural buys due to credit expansion and stable asset quality. FMCG is positioned for a 6-month mean reversion rebound after underperformance.
