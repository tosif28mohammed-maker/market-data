# Can Zudio Sustain Trent's Growth?
स्रोत: transcript: Groww · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5wNmprpBt3A · Gemini ने पढ़ा 2026-11-28 09:17 UTC

## Point-by-point
- Trent Ltd ka stock 86x P/E (86 times earnings) par trade kar raha hai, jahan earnings growth slow hone par P/E multiple de-rating (50% contraction) ka severe risk hota hai.
- Company ki sales growth rate me tezz giraavat dekhi gayi hai: FY23 me sales growth 83% thi jo FY26 me ghat kar 17% reh gayi (transcript galat ho sakti hai - numbers FY23 se FY26), halanki EBITDA margin 14% se badhkar 19% hua hai.
- Zudio store expansion: FY22 se FY26 tak Zudio stores 4x (char guna) aur Westside stores 1.5x bade. September tak Zudio ke 999 stores aur Westside ke 311 stores ho chuke the.
- Existing stores ki performance: Q2 me Like-for-Like (LFL) sales growth sirf mid-single digits (lagbhag 5%) rahi, jiska matlab hai ki revenue growth ka mukhya driver naye stores ki kholna hai, na ki purane stores ki organic growth.
- Expansion trajectory risk: Q2 me khule 85% naye Zudio stores chhote shahron (Tier 2/3/4) me khule hain, jinka payback period aur store maturation time bade shahron ke muqable lamba hota hai.
- High P/E retail stocks ko analyze karne ke liye 3 mukhya metrics track karna zaroori hai: (1) Total Store Count expansion speed, (2) Existing store Like-for-Like (LFL) sales growth, aur (3) Naye stores se aane wala incremental profit margin.

## Niyam
- [new/yes] Avoid buying or exit high-valuation retail stocks (P/E > 85x) when top-line growth decelerates sharply (Sales CAGR dropping from >80% to <20%) and Like-for-Like (LFL) store sales growth drops to mid-single digits (~5%). | entry: Avoid fresh long entries or trim existing positions when trailing P/E > 85x AND quarterly revenue growth decelerates to < 20% while LFL store sales growth drops to <= 5% | exit: Exit position if quarterly earnings growth fails to support 85x P/E multiple leading to P/E contraction | stop: number nahi diya
- [new/yes] Retail Sector Tier 2/3 Expansion Drag Filter: Apply a valuation discount / caution tag when >85% of new store additions are concentrated in Tier-2/3 small towns and LFL sales growth is <= 5%. | entry: Do not pay >50x P/E multiple for retail expansion stories where >85% of new store openings are in Tier 2/3 small towns unless LFL (Like-for-Like) store sales growth exceeds 10% | exit: number nahi diya | stop: number nahi diya
