## Point-by-point
- Sensex vs Wealthex Performance: Sensex ki earnings growth pichle 4 studies se single digit me rahi hai. G = R (Growth in earnings = Return on stock) niyam ke mutabiq Sensex ko beat karne ke liye superior earnings profile wale stocks ka portfolio banana zaruri hai. Wealthex ne Sensex ko 107% se outperform kiya (Mar-16 se Mar-21).
- Sector Wealth Creation Ranking (2016-2021): Financials sector top spot par raha (INR 17,365 bn, 24.5% share, 26% Price CAGR, 18% PAT CAGR, 2021 P/E 27x, RoE 13%). Technology 2nd spot par (INR 15,192 bn, 21.5% share, 18% Price CAGR, 8% PAT CAGR, RoE 26%). Consumer & Retail 3rd spot par (INR 12,541 bn, 17.7% share, 26% Price CAGR, 16% PAT CAGR). Top losers share me Autos aur Pharma rahe.
- PSU vs Private Ownership Performance: 2016-21 me PSUs ka pradarshan sabse kharab raha. Top 100 Wealth Creators me sirf 2 PSUs (Gujarat Gas aur Indraprastha Gas) aaye jinka Wealth Creation share <1% (0.8%) tha. Private sector ka share 99.2% tha. 5-year PAT CAGR PSUs ka 29% vs Private 18%, Price CAGR PSUs ka 37% vs Private 25%.
- Market Cap Rank Crossover Matrix (2016-2021): Mega = Top 100, Mid = Ranks 101-250, Mini = Rank 251+. 2016 me 2,680 Mini stocks me se sirf 1 Mega bana (Adani Transmission, 93% Price CAGR), 20 Mid bane (46% Price CAGR). 150 Mid stocks me se 12 Mega bane (34% Price CAGR), 77 Mid hi rahe (16% CAGR), 61 Mini ban gaye (-10% CAGR). 100 Mega stocks me se 74 Mega rahe (12% CAGR), 25 Mid bane (0% CAGR), 1 Mini bana (-21% CAGR). Benchmark Sensex CAGR 14% tha.
- Mid-to-Mega Investment Hunting Ground: Market Cap Rank 101-250 (150 stocks) sabse potent hunting ground hai. Next 5 years me inme se 12-15 stocks (8-10% strike rate) Mega rank (Top 100) me cross over karke 34-43% average price CAGR deliver karte hain.
- Valuation Metrics Performance (2016-2021): Payback Ratio < 1x (Market Cap / Next 5-year cumulative PAT) sabse solid multi-bagger formula hai (13 stocks, 40% Price CAGR, RoE 10%). P/E < 10x ne 35% Price CAGR diya. P/B < 1x ne 36% Price CAGR diya. P/S < 1x ne 33% Price CAGR diya.
- PEG Ratio < 1x Outperformance Rule: PEG = Trailing 12M P/E / Future 5-year PAT CAGR. PEG < 0.5x ne sabse highest return diya (7 stocks, 67% Price CAGR, 39% PAT CAGR, 22% RoE 2021). PEG 0.5-1.0x ne 33% Price CAGR, PEG 1-2x ne 26% CAGR, PEG 2-3x ne 19% CAGR, PEG >3x ne 23% CAGR diya.
- Wealth Destroyers Analysis (2016-2021): Total Wealth Destruction INR 10,952 bn (INR 11 trillion) tha, jo Total Wealth Created ka 15% hai. Financials sector top wealth destroyer bhi bana (INR 2,239 bn, 20% share), visheshkar Public Sector Banks ke kaaran. Top wealth destroyers: Vodafone Idea (INR 1,362 bn), Coal India (INR 994 bn), Sun Pharma (INR 532 bn), ONGC (INR 512 bn), Yes Bank (INR 292 bn). Top 10 me se 5 PSUs the.
- All-round Wealth Creators Triad Ranking: Biggest (absolute wealth), Fastest (% price CAGR), aur Consistent (number of years outperforming benchmark) teeno ranks ko jodkar composite rank nikalne par top overall outperformers milte hain (jaise Adani Enterprises #1, Adani Transmission #2, Bajaj Finance #3, Bajaj Finserv #4, Reliance Industries #5).
