## Point-by-point
- 25-saal ke data (1995-2020) mein 1995 ki top 500 companies me se sirf 100 companies ne Sensex ke 9.2% Price CAGR ko outperform kiya.
- Top 25 Fastest Wealth Creators ka 25-saal ka average Price CAGR 23% raha, jabki PAT CAGR 22% raha.
- Infosys 30% Price CAGR (688x multiple) aur 33% PAT CAGR (1256x multiple) ke saath sabse tez wealth creator bana.
- Reliance Industries ne INR 6,307 billion ka Net Wealth Create karke sabse bada wealth creator ka khitab jeeta, uske baad Hindustan Unilever (INR 4,893 billion).
- Kotak Mahindra Bank sabse consistent wealth creator raha, jisne 23 me se 21 rolling 3-year periods me Sensex ko beat kiya. Iska combined All-Round rank (Fastest 14 + Biggest 5 + Consistent 1 = 20) sabse kam tha.
- 63% top 100 wealth creators consumer-facing businesses se aaye, jinaka total wealth creation mix 68% (INR 12 trillion) tha.
- 1995 ki top 100 mega-cap companies me se sirf 32% companies 2020 me mega-cap rahi, 20% mid-cap me giri aur 45% mini-cap me fisal gayi (negative return -3% CAGR).
- Mini (rank >250) se Mega (top 100) crossover karne wali companies ne 24% CAGR diya, jabki Mid (101-250) se Mega crossover ne 21% CAGR diya.
- Sirf 3 sectors — Consumer/Retail, IT aur Paints — ne picchle 25 saal ke har ek saal me sector RoE > 13% maintain rakha.
- Corporate Profit to GDP jab historical bottom 2.3%-2.8% par stagnate hota hai, wahan se long-term market earnings rebound ki shuruaat hoti hai.
- 10-year G-Sec yield 12-14% se girkar 6-8% aane par Sensex average P/E 18x se badhkar 27x tak pahunch gaya.
- Market Cap to GDP ratio 100% (long-term average 60%) hona broad market me extreme valuation froth ko darshata hai.
