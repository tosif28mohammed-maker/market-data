## Point-by-point
- Market Relief & Dips: Recent market rebound provides relief after a relentless slowdown, but global/US markets can bring curveballs. Investors must keep cash/liquidity ('powder dry') to take advantage of sudden dips.
- NVIDIA & AI Tech Leaders: When top global market leaders in tech/AI undergo severe corrections (~45-50% drop from peaks) due to regulatory/accounting scrutiny, do not attempt to guess the exact bottom. Practice staggered tranche buying (e.g., 25-30% allocation at a time) using preserved liquidity.
- Tata Steel - APL Apollo Deal: Promoters selling out at peak valuations can leave minority shareholders holding underperforming larger group stocks. Historically underperforming parent companies (like Tata Steel) may drag return expectations.
- Bank Nifty & Financials: Sharp Bank Nifty rallies are often driven by F&O positioning, algo trading, and short covering rather than pure cash equity inflows. Investors should focus on stock-specific fundamental strength.
- PSU Bank Re-rating Opportunity: PSU Banks trading at single-digit P/E multiples (< 10x) present a potential value re-rating opportunity if valuation multiples expand into double digits (40-50% expansion).
- IT Sector Bearish Stance: Maintain avoidance / underweight stance on traditional Indian IT stocks as structural business relevance diminishes amidst global tech shifts, ignoring temporary short-covering bounces.
