## Point-by-point
- Uncommon Profit Concept: Business me competitive forces standard returns ko cost of capital tak ghata deti hain. Sustained RoE > 15% (India me Cost of Equity threshold) ko Uncommon Profit ya Economic Value Creation kaha jata hai.
- Uncommon Profit Volume Formula: Volume = Length (saal) x Breadth (ploughed-back capital) x Height (RoE - CoE spread). Ye QGL (Quality, Growth, Longevity) model se match karta hai.
- Emerging Value Creators: 25 saal se kam umar wali companies jo pehli baar 15% RoE threshold breach karti hain, PAT >= Rs 10 crore ho, non-cyclical ho aur P/E <= 20x ho. Inka 5-saal average price CAGR 41% rehta hai.
- Emerging Amplifiers: Emerging screening me 3 filters jodne par (High Profit Pool sector, B2C/Consumer Advantage, Top-3 Market Share) 5-saal ka price CAGR 41% se badhkar 65% ho jata hai (Sensex se +43% relative outperformance).
- Enduring Value Creators Screen: Continuous 10 saal tak RoE > 15%, 3-year PAT CAGR > 15%, positive 5-year RoE Delta (RoE_t > RoE_{t-5}), aur Trailing P/E <= 30x. 2008-2013 bear market me is filter ne 24% CAGR return diya (vs Sensex 4%).
- Market Outperformance Filter: Wealth Creation screening ke liye absolute wealth growth ke saath-saath 5-year stock price CAGR > Sensex CAGR hona zaroori filter hai.
- Macro Bottoming Filter: Corporate Profit-to-GDP ratio <= 4.5% (10-year low) aur Market Cap-to-GDP <= 61% (55-61% range) indicate karta hai ki market reasonably valued/bottomed out hai aur long-term risk-reward highly favorable hai.
