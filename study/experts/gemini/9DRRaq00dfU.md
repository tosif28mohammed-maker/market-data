## Point-by-point
- Nikhil Zelawat and Vivek Bajaj discuss building an automated, AI-driven Trading Journal and Decision Support System using Claude Desktop, Dhan API, TradingView library, and Python/VS Code.
- System Architecture: Connects broker account (e.g. Dhan) via API token and Client ID to automatically pull trade history, match buys/sells using a FIFO (First-In, First-Out) engine, and group split executions by Order ID.
- Mindset Shift to Journal-First Trading: Decisions are made pre-market or post-market through a rulebook interface. Market hours are restricted to execution only (15-30 minute focused window) to eliminate impulsive intraday trading.
- Time Window Execution Rules: More than 70% of breakout trades taken in cash market before 9:30 AM fail to make new highs. Use 9:15 AM - 9:30 AM exclusively to book profits / sell into strength on existing positions, while deferring fresh long entries until after 9:30 AM (or 10:00 AM).
- Stagnant Position Exit Rule: For swing trading, if a stock is held for 7 to 8 trading days without expanding/moving upward (and SL is not hit), trim or exit the position to free up locked capital.
- Multi-Tier Trailing Stop-Loss: Set initial SL at 5%. When trade moves to +5% profit (1R), trail SL to Breakeven (0%). At +2R (+10% profit), trail further. Alternatively, trigger exit if price closes below the 5-day moving average low (5 DMA low).
- Portfolio Position Sizing Limit: Maximum allocation per stock should not exceed 5% of total portfolio capital. If no stop-loss is set manually, the system auto-enforces a default hard stop-loss of 5% to 7%.
- 5-Day Benchmark Rotation Audit: Compare individual stock performance against Nifty 500 every 5 trading days. Trim 50% capital from laggards/underperformers relative to Nifty 500 and reallocate to high-alpha movers.
- MAE / MFE Performance Simulation: Calculate Maximum Favorable Excursion (MFE) and Maximum Adverse Excursion (MAE) monthly to optimize trailing rules and profit-taking targets (e.g., adjusting target captures between 15% and 25% depending on market momentum).
