## Point-by-point
- Jayant Mundhra aur Vivek Bajaj ki charcha me Indian macro economy, state finances, foreign direct investment (FDI), aur corporate governance ke forensic indicators par detail me baat hui.
- Howard Marks ki book 'The Most Important Thing' ke aadhar par contrarian investing ka niyam: Jab share deep fear zone me gire aur fundamental research se turnaround conviction mile, tabhi enter karo.
- Contrarian position me agar price 25%-40% aur girta hai lekin fundamental thesis milestones intact hain, toh staggered averaging karo. Agar thesis me koi milestone fail ho jaye, toh 20% loss par bhi turant exit karo.
- IPO forensic analysis filter: IPO aane se pehle trade receivables to revenue ratio check karo. Agar receivables total revenue ka 80%-89% (jaise Sunshine Pictures me 89%) hain aur OCF negative/zero hai, toh IPO me enter mat karo.
- Promoter related party transaction filter: Direct sales ya commissions jo sister entities (jaise Ola Financial Services ya ANI Tech) se show ki gayi hon par cash bank account me na aaya ho (uncollected receivables), wo revenue quality trap hai.
- Product quality & defect metric filter: EV / Automobile / Manufacturing companies ka Warranty Expense as a % of Revenue check karo. Agar warranty expense revenue ka >5% hai (jaise Ola Electric 8.2% vs Ather 1.7%), toh quality defect risk high hone ke karan stock avoid karo.
- Legal expenses vs Infrastructure Rent filter: Agar company ka annual legal & professional fee spending unke base lease/rent infrastructure spending se zyada hai (jaise Rs 111 Cr legal vs Rs 86 Cr rent), ya legal cost per unit sold > Rs 2,500 hai, toh hidden litigation/customer dispute risk ke karan stock me long mat bano.
- State level capex availability filter (RBI State Finances Report): Un states ke infrastructure/industrial projects me focus karo jahan state government ke paas interest, subsidy, pension aur salary ke baad development/incentives ke liye >30% budget bachta hai (jaise Gujarat 42%, Tamil Nadu 33% vs Punjab 11%).
- Solar sector Domestic Content Requirement (DCR) filter: Government tenders aur PM Surya Ghar scheme me DCR solar cells zaroori hain jo non-DCR imported cells se ~60% premium par bikte hain. DCR cell manufacturing capacity wali companies (Waaree, Vikram Solar, Premier Energies) tracking list me rakhein.
