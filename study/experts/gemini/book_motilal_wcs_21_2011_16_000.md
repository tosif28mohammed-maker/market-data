## Point-by-point
- Kitaab/Report ka mukhya dhyan 'What to buy' (stock selection) ke saath-saath 'How much to buy' (stock allocation / position sizing) par hai.
- Kelly's Formula for Equities: Position sizing ka formula f = (Up - Dq) / U hai, jahan U = Upside %, p = Upside ki probability, D = Downside %, q = Downside ki probability. Equity me iska matlab hai: Asymmetric payoff (bada upside, chhota downside) + High probability wale setups par bada bet lagao.
- Edge Formula: Edge = Up - Dq (expected value). Edge non-mathematical roop se analytical advantage (superior fundamental framework/research) se aata hai. Agar edge nahi hai to bet mat lagao.
- Focused Portfolio Framework: Over-diversification (40+ shares) se returns benchmark ke barabar (modest) ho jate hain. Optimal portfolio 15-20 shares ka hota hai jo risk ko diversify karta hai aur return ko multiply karta hai.
- Allocation limits: Focused portfolio me kisi bhi ek share me max weight 10%, min weight 3%, aur company ke total equity capitalization ka max 5% hi hold karna chahiye.
- CAP (Confidence-Adjusted Payoff) Allocation Model: Pehle shares ko expected 3-5 saal ke upside % ke hisab se rank karo, phir us par 0-100% ka Confidence Factor apply karo (CAP = Upside x Confidence Factor). Higher CAP wale shares ko zyada allocation do.
- Market Cap Crossovers: Mini-to-Mid (Rank >300 se Rank 101-300) crossover stocks ne 5 saal me 39% CAGR price return diya. Mid-to-Mega (Rank 101-300 se Top 100) crossover stocks ne 31% CAGR return diya.
- Fastest Wealth Creators Pattern: Base year me market cap < Rs 2,000 crore (< Rs 20 Billion), scalable niche ya bada sector, hyper PAT growth (>30-50%), aur low P/E se high P/E multi-fold expansion.
- Consistent Wealth Creators Pattern: Consumer-facing discretionary brand stocks (Asian Paints, Titan, Dabur) regular 10-year Price CAGR > 20% aur PAT CAGR > 15-20% dete hain, chahe purchase price kuch bhi ho.
- Cyclical Stocks Exit Rule: Cyclical shares (Metals, Mining, Oil & Gas, Capital Goods) me 'When to Sell' (peak par bechna) utna hi zaroori hai jitna 'When to Buy'. Peaking ke baad hold karne par 50-90% tak wealth destroy hoti hai.
