# Motilal Oswal 22nd Annual Wealth Creation Study (2012-2017) - CAP & GAP Power of Longevity

## Point-by-point
- Longevity of earnings growth (GAP) and competitive advantage (CAP) are the primary drivers of long-term outperformance in equity investments.
- Cost of Equity threshold for Indian equities is benchmarked at 15% (long-term Sensex/Nifty return CAGR). RoE above 15% defines Competitive Advantage Period (CAP).
- Benchmark PAT growth rate threshold is benchmarked at 15%. Rolling 5-year PAT CAGR > 15% with zero negative PAT years defines Growth Advantage Period (GAP).
- Porter's 5 Forces Scoring Model evaluates industry structure: Inter-firm rivalry, Threat of entrants, Substitutes, Customer power, and Supplier power scored 0, 0.5, or 1 each. Total Score > 2.5 = Favorable Industry; <= 2.5 = Unfavorable Industry.
- Corporate strategy is categorized as Favorable (Differentiated, Low-cost, Focus) vs Unfavorable (Stuck-in-the-middle).
- CAP Matrix combines Industry Structure and Company Strategy into 4 quadrants: F-F (Longest CAP, median 11 years), U-F (Long CAP, median 9 years), F-U (Short CAP, median 7 years), U-U (Shortest CAP, median 6 years).
- GAP Matrix combines Industry Growth (PAT CAGR > 15%) and Company Growth Mindset (Aggressive expansion, inorganic M&A, operating/financial leverage) into 4 quadrants: H-H (Highest PAT growth, median 39%), L-H (37%), H-L (31%), L-L (25%).
- CAP-GAP Confluence Filter selects stocks at the intersection of F-F/U-F CAP and H-H GAP, yielding the highest median PAT CAGR (39%) and Price CAGR (36%).
- Cyclical high GAP setups generate supernormal short-term profit growth (PAT CAGR 40-150%) but sustain only for short durations (5-6 years), whereas Secular high GAP setups sustain for 10-20 years.

## Niyam
- [new/yes] CAP (Competitive Advantage Period) Moat Screening Filter: Select companies maintaining annual RoE > 15% (Cost of Equity threshold) with Favorable Strategy in a Favorable Industry (Porter Score > 2.5) | entry: Buy stocks when RoE > 15% AND Porter Industry Score > 2.5 AND Corporate Strategy is Differentiated/Low-cost/Focus (F-F Quadrant) | exit: Exit or liquidate long positions when annual RoE drops below 15% (End of CAP) or when disruptive competition/capital misallocation degrades moat | stop: Annual RoE falling below 15%
- [new/yes] GAP (Growth Advantage Period) Multi-Year Earnings Momentum Filter: Buy companies with 5-Year Rolling PAT CAGR > 15% and zero annual PAT declines | entry: Buy stocks where 5-year rolling PAT CAGR > 15% AND current year PAT > previous year PAT (no profit drop in any single year) | exit: Exit when 5-year rolling PAT CAGR drops below 15% or when any annual PAT drops year-on-year | stop: Annual YoY PAT decline or 5-year PAT CAGR < 15%
- [new/yes] Porter's Five Forces Sector Selection & Exclusion Rule: Exclude sectors with total score <= 2.5; Long only in sectors with total score >= 3.0 | entry: Filter universe for stocks operating in sectors with Porter Five Forces Score >= 3.0 (e.g. Cigarettes 5.0, Paints 5.0, FMCG 4.5, 2-Wheelers 4.0, Private Banks 3.0, Pharma 3.0, Cement 3.0) | exit: Completely avoid or exit all long positions in stocks belonging to sectors with Porter Score <= 2.5 (e.g. Capital Goods 2.0, Real Estate 2.0, Construction 1.5, Steel 2.0, Power 1.5, Telecom 3.0 if inter-firm rivalry spikes) | stop: Sector downgrade to Porter Score <= 2.5
- [new/yes] CAP-GAP Confluence Outperformer Setup: Buy stocks matching CAP F-F/U-F + GAP H-H with PAT >= Rs 500 Crore | entry: Buy stocks meeting 3 criteria simultaneously: (1) RoE > 15% with Favorable Strategy (CAP F-F or U-F), (2) Industry PAT CAGR > 15% + High Growth Mindset (GAP H-H), and (3) Cumulative 20-year or trailing cumulative PAT >= INR 5 Billion (Rs 500 Crore) | exit: Exit when either RoE drops below 15% OR PAT growth falls below 15% for 2 consecutive years | stop: RoE < 15% or PAT CAGR < 15%
- [new/yes] Cyclical vs Secular GAP Horizon & Exit Strategy: Restrict Cyclical High-GAP positions to max 5-6 years; Hold Secular High-GAP positions for 10-20 years | entry: Identify high-GAP stock setup (PAT CAGR > 35%). If Secular (FMCG, Private Banking, IT, Pharma), set target horizon to 10-20 years | exit: For Cyclicals: Exit mandatory after 5-6 years or at first sign of PAT growth deceleration below 15%.
