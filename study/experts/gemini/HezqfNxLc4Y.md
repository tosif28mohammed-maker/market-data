## Point-by-point
- Traditional indicators like Moving Averages, MACD, and RSI serve as foundational tools, but evolving market regimes require advanced and custom indicators for improved edge.
- Dhan platform provides over 150+ indicators including institutional and custom indicators like Dhan Half Trend, Dhan Alpha Trend, SSL Channel, UT Bot, and Dhan Super Scalper.
- Dhan Half Trend & SSL Channel define dynamic range boundaries; when price moves beyond these channels, mean-reversion trades can be taken as price snaps back inside the range.
- UT Bot indicator modifies candle colors and generates Buy/Sell labels using ATR-based trailing stops (Key Value: 1, ATR Period: 10).
- Dhan Super Scalper is specifically built for 1-min and 5-min index scalping (Nifty/BankNifty) combining ATR bands, Fast EMA (21), Slow EMA (55), and momentum oscillators to trigger Golden Buy (blue label) and Golden Sell (yellow label) signals.
