## Point-by-point
- Kitaab me equity investing ko speculation se alag karne ke liye intrinsic value aur Margin of Safety ka istemal kiya gaya hai; intrinsic value ke liye minimum Cost of Equity (CoE) 13% rakha gaya hai.
- Lindy Effect Framework: Purani companies (jo alag-alag market cycles se guzari hain) ke aage bhi tiki rehne aur perform karne ki sambhavna nayi companies se zyada hoti hai.
- Great, Good, Gruesome Classification: Great companies me RoE > Cost of Equity (13%) aur low capital intensity hoti hai; Good companies me high growth ke sath high capital re-investment chahiye hota hai; Gruesome companies (jaise airlines) me capital intensity high aur RoE low hoti hai, jinhe hamesha avoid karna chahiye.
- Emergence & Endurance Model: Jab koi company pehli baar ya kaafi samay baad RoE > 13% (Cost of Equity) cross karti hai (Emergence) aur usko sustain karti hai (Endurance), tab multi-bagger returns bante hain (jaise Titan 2003 me).
- Terms of Trade (ToT): ToT = Debtors / Creditors. Jitna kam ratio hoga, company ka bargaining power utna mazboot hoga aur Operating Cash Flow (OCF) / RoCE me sudhaar aayega.
- Common-size P&L Analysis: Total Sales ko 100% maan kar expense heads (jaise Employee Cost % of Sales) ke trend ko track karke EBITDA margin expansion ko pehle se pehchana ja sakta hai (jaise Bata 2005-2010 case study me employee cost 25.3% se gir kar 13.6% hui).
- Du Pont 3-Factor 5-Year Quantitative Screen: Top 500 stocks me 5 saal ke dauran PAT Margin expansion + Asset Turnover expansion + Financial Leverage < 2.0 hone par portfolio ne Sensex (1%) ke muqable 15% CAGR return diya.
- Winner Category & Category Winner Model: Sector revenue growth >= 1.5x nominal GDP growth + Consolidated market structure wale sectors se top moat leader ko reasonable valuation (P/E <= 21x) par khareedna.
