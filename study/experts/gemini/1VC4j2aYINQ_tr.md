## Point-by-point
- Subah 9:15 AM se 9:45 AM / 10:00 AM tak market me high volatility aur powerplay jaisa momentum hota hai.
- 10:30 AM / 11:00 AM se 1:00 PM / 1:30 PM ke beech bazaar shant hota hai jab tak European markets nahi khulte.
- Is shant window (10:30 AM se 1:30 PM) me koi bhi Index (Nifty, Bank Nifty, Sensex, Midcap Nifty, Fin Nifty) me option selling ki ja sakti hai, chahe expiry day ho ya normal day.
- Condition for Entry: 10-minute ya 15-minute timeframe chart par ADX indicator neeche gir raha ho aur preferably 20 ke neeche ho subah 10:30 AM - 11:00 AM ke dauran.
- Jab ADX girta hai to bazaar me trending movement nahi hoti aur range-bound period me option premium decay se profit banaya ja sakta hai.
