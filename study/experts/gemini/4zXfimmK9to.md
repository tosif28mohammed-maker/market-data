# Power Equipment Sector Order-to-Cash Quality & High Valuation Caution Filter

## Point-by-point
- India's power transmission grid expansion is backed by a Rs 9 lakh Cr+ investment plan through 2032, driving strong order growth for power equipment manufacturers (transformers, substations, switchgear, HVDC systems).
- High order backlog and revenue growth (e.g., >50% order growth) do not automatically translate into cash generation due to long execution cycles and working capital build-up.
- Operating Cash Flow vs Net Profit divergence: For instance, Hitachi Energy reported H1 FY27 Net Profit of Rs 613 Cr but Operating Cash Flow of only Rs 91 Cr (-89.6% YoY) due to working-capital accumulation.
- Valuations in the power equipment sector can reach elevated trailing P/E multiples (~75x to 111x), increasing risk if project execution or billing is delayed.
- Investor evaluation matrix: Verify how efficiently Orders translate into Revenue, then Net Profit, and finally actual Operating Cash Flow.
