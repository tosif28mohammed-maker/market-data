## Point-by-point
- Direct vs Regular Mutual Fund Plans: Regular plan mein distributor ko ~0.7%-1% commission jaata hai jabki Direct plan mein koi distributor commission nahi lagta, jisse 1% extra amount invest hota hai aur long term mein behtar returns milte hain.
- SEBI Registered Investment Advisors (RIA): SEBI RIAs fee-only model par kaam karte hain aur unhe sirf Direct Plans recommend karne ki anumati hoti hai (commission lena prohibited hai).
- Total Expense Ratio (TER) Cap: SEBI ne equity mutual funds par maximum Total Expense Ratio cap 2.1% tay kiya hai.
- Growth vs IDCW (Dividend) Option: Growth plan mein underlying stocks se milne wala dividend fund mein reinvest ho jaata hai jo tax-efficient hota hai; IDCW (Income Distribution cum Capital Withdrawal) option mein dividend payout hota hai jo passive income ke liye useful hai par tax-inefficient hota hai.
- Exit Load Rule Example: Parag Parikh Flexi Cap Fund mein 1 saal se pehle exit karne par 2% exit load aur 1 se 2 saal ke beech exit karne par 1% exit load lagta hai.
- Mutual Fund Redemption Liquidity: First half mein sell request daalne par T+1 working day aur second half mein daalne par T+2 working days mein paisa bank account mein credit ho jaata hai.
