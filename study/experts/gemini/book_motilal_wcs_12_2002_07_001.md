## Point-by-point
- Sensex valuation scenario model based on Earnings Yield to Bond Yield ratio fixed at 0.7.
- Fair Market P/E is calculated using formula: P/E = 1 / (0.7 * G-Sec Yield). For instance, at 9.0% G-Sec yield P/E is 16x, at 8.0% yield P/E is 18x, and at 7.0% yield P/E is 20x.
- Target Sensex level is calculated as EPS * Fair P/E, tested across EPS growth projections of 15%, 20%, 25%, and 30%.
- Market sentiment filter: High purchase price relative to earnings yield reduces margin of safety; market upside is driven by high liquidity, corporate earnings growth, and falling interest rates.
- Appendices I, II, and III present historical 5-year performance data (2002-2007) for Top 100 Biggest and Fastest Wealth Creators, tracking metrics like Price CAGR, PAT CAGR, Sales CAGR, ROE, and P/E expansion.
