## Point-by-point
- Blue Chip Buy Signal #1: Kharido tab jab stock ka Dividend Yield uske 10-saal ke median Dividend Yield se zyada ho AUR stock ka P/E uske 10-saal ke median P/E se kam ho.
- Blue Chip Buy Signal #2: Kharido tab jab Blue Chip stock ka Dividend Yield 3% se zyada ho jaaye (chunki Indian market me average yield ~1.5% hota hai).
- Potential Blue Chip 5-Factor Screen: Un emerging companies ko chunna jinme pichle 5 saal me: (1) Uninterrupted dividend diya ho, (2) Kam se kam 3 saal EPS bada ho, (3) Kam se kam 2 saal dividend bada ho, (4) Har saal RoE >= 15% raha ho, aur (5) 5-saal PAT CAGR >= 10% ho.
- Recognized vs Potential Blue Chip classification: Market Cap > Rs 10,000 Crore (INR 100b) wali dividend-paying quality companies ko Recognized Blue Chips mana gaya, jabki Market Cap <= Rs 10,000 Crore wali companies ko Potential Blue Chips mana gaya.
- Blue Chip Exit Rule: Blue chip me selling ka mauka tabhi aata hai jab extreme overvaluation manic state me P/E multi-fold (jaise 10x se 40x) ho jaye aur Dividend Yield gir kar 0.3% tak aa jaye. Valuation mean reversion hone par wapas buy karein.
