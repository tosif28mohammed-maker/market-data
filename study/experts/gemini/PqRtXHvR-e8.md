## Point-by-point
- Stock Price & CEO Resignation Context: NVIDIA stock fell ~36% from its October peak of $243 following rumors and uncertainty regarding CEO Jensen Huang's resignation.
- Q3 FY27 Earnings Performance: NVIDIA reported record revenue of $115.8 Billion (+103% YoY), driven by Data Center revenue of $107.6 Billion (+110% YoY) and Operating Profit of $77.82 Billion (+115% YoY).
- Market Share & Competition: In standalone Data Center GPUs, NVIDIA holds a ~92% market share. However, when custom AI chips (Google TPUs, AWS Trainium) are included, NVIDIA's share of total AI compute is estimated at 62.5%, with Google at 25.4%.
- Cash Conversion & Receivables Risk: Operating Cash Flow / Operating Profit ratio declined from 77.3% in 9M FY26 to 63.0% in 9M FY27. Accounts Receivable rose 29% QoQ to $81.0 Billion, pushing Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) up to 64 days.
- Supply & Capacity Commitments: Future supply and capacity commitments increased 40% QoQ to $390 Billion, primarily driven by multi-year memory procurement deals ($203 Billion scheduled for FY28).
- Gross Margin Outlook: Gross margins are expected to compress from ~75% currently to 71-72% in Q4 FY27 and 72-73% in FY28 due to product transitions.
- 4 Key Investor Watchpoints for NVIDIA: (1) Receivables and collection cycles, (2) Inventory conversion and shipment timelines, (3) Hyperscaler capex vs operating cash flow sustainability, (4) Supply commitments vs realized sales.
