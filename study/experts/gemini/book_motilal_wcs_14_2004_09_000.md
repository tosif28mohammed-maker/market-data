## Point-by-point
- 14th Wealth Creation Study (2004-2009) me Indian market ke 100 sabse bade wealth creators ka analysis kiya gaya hai.
- Fastest Wealth Creators Screen: Top fastest stocks me se 90% me kam se kam 2 shartein poori hoti hain: (1) Base Market Cap < Rs 400 Crore (Rs 4b), (2) Base P/E < 10x, (3) 5-Year PAT CAGR > 35%.
- RoE Sweet-Spot Rule: Indian market me sabse zyada Price CAGR (29-33%) un companies ne diya jinka base RoE 5% se 15% ke beech tha (low to moderate initial RoE), kyunki market inki profitability improvement ko pehle se price-in nahi karta.
- Winner Category Selection Criteria: Unhi sectors/categories ko chuno jinme: (a) Revenue CAGR >= 18% (Nominal GDP growth 12% ka 1.5x) ya Volume CAGR >= 10.5% (Real GDP 7% ka 1.5x), (b) High to medium industry consolidation ho (tukde-tukde me banta hua sector na ho), (c) Global shocks aur regulatory return caps se mukt ho.
- Category Winner & Entry Barrier Rule: Winner category ki market leader company me tabhi invest karo jab uske paas strong entry barrier ho. High-quality consumer/brand companies ka 10-Year Average Adjusted RoCE > 40% hota hai (Adjusted RoCE = [EBIT - 8% CCE Income] / [Capital Employed - Cash & Cash Equivalents]).
- Small Cap Edge: 74% wealth creators ka base market cap < Rs 5,000 Crore (Rs 50b) tha. Small base + High PAT growth (>30%) = Maximum wealth creation.
