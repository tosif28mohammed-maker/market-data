## Point-by-point
- Wealth Creation Definition & Methodology: Top 100 Wealth Creators (MOSL-100) ko last 5 saal (2006-2011) me dilutive market cap addition ke basis par analyze kiya gaya (minimum INR 1 billion net wealth creation threshold).
- Top Wealth Creators (2006-2011): Reliance Industries sabse bada wealth creator bana (INR 1,742 billion created, 21% Price CAGR), iske baad TCS (INR 1,379 billion) aur SBI (INR 1,075 billion). Top 10 wealth creators ka share total wealth creation me 76% (2003) se ghat kar 42% (2011) ho gaya, jo broader wealth creation dikhata hai.
- Fastest Wealth Creators: Sanwaria Agro 119% Price CAGR (50x price jump) ke saath #1 fastest wealth creator bana, followed by Adani Enterprises (86% CAGR, 22x) aur Bhushan Steel (64% CAGR, 12x). Report note karti hai ki zyadatar fastest wealth creators (transitory multi-baggers) agle 3 saal me 30-98% peak value lose kar dete hain (sirf Cipla exception raha).
- Most Consistent Wealth Creators: Kotak Mahindra Bank 10-year Price CAGR of 47% ke saath top consistent wealth creator bana (10 consecutive studies me featured), followed by Sun Pharma (33% CAGR) aur Asian Paints (31% CAGR). Private sector financials aur consumer-facing stocks consistent performance me aage hain.
- Wealthex vs BSE Sensex: Wealthex (top 100 index) ne 5-year period me 18% point-to-point CAGR return diya, jabki Sensex ne 12% return diya. Wealthex ka EPS CAGR 18% raha (Sensex 14%) aur standard deviation 13% raha (Sensex 16%), jo lower volatility aur higher return prove karta hai.
- Industry Trends: Financials sector sabse bada wealth creator sector bana (24% share, INR 5,194 billion wealth created, 28% Price CAGR, 25% PAT CAGR), overtaking Oil & Gas (14% share). Consumer, Auto, aur Healthcare ka share bhi steadily grow hua.
- PSU vs Private Performance: Private companies ne PSUs ko significanly outperform kiya (Private Sales CAGR 28% vs PSU 16%, PAT CAGR 24% vs PSU 14%, Price CAGR 21% vs PSU 14%). PSUs ka wealth creation share 2004 me 51% se ghat kar 2011 me 24% reh gaya.
- Company Age & Market Cap Impact: 0-10 saal purani companies ne highest Price CAGR (26%) aur PAT CAGR (36%) diya lekin size of wealth small raha (2%). Base market cap < INR 10 billion wali companies fastest speed dengi lekin risk zyada rahega.
- Earnings Power & Valuations: Long-term stock returns clear correlation dikhate hain PAT growth se. Super-normal PAT growth (>30-60%) focus karne par P/E de-rating ho sakti hai kyunki cyclical stocks high-growth bucket me hote hain.
- Blue Chip Investing Concept & 6 Quality Filters: Blue Chip investing low risk me superior wealth create karti hai. Quality analysis ke liye 6-screen filter set kiya gaya hai.
- Valuation Framework (DDM & Dividend Yield Angle): Intrinsic value calculation DDM model P = D / (k - g) par based hai. Equation P/E = Payout / (k - g) se pata chalta hai ki P/E directly payout ratio, RoE, aur expected growth par dependent hota hai. High payout companies ka dividend yield floor P/E ratio determine karta hai.
- Infosys vs HUL / Asian Paints Case Study: Infosys ka PAT growth (15% CAGR) HUL (8% CAGR) se zyada raha, lekin low payout ratio (36% vs HUL 85%) aur high cash balance ke karan Infosys ka PE (22x) HUL (27x) se lower valuation multiple par trade karta raha.

## Niyam
- [new/yes] Blue Chip Stock Selection ke liye 6-Screen Quality Filter: Un stocks me long-term position lo jo in 6 sharton ko poora karein: (1) 20 saal tak bina ruke continuous dividend payout, (2) pichle 12 saalon me se kam se kam 5 saal dividend me growth/step-up, (3) pichle 12 saalon me se kam se kam 7 saal net profit (PAT) me positive growth, (4) pichle 12 saalon ka average Return on Equity (RoE) >= 15%, (5) kam se kam 5 million (50 lakh) outstanding equity shares, aur (6) minimum 80 institutional investors (MFs/FIIs/Insurance) ki holding. | entry: Jab stock sabhi 6 screens (20-yr uninterrupted div, div step-up in >=5/12 yrs, PAT growth in >=7/12 yrs, 12-yr avg RoE >= 15%, >= 5M shares, >= 80 institutional holders) ko poora kare. | exit: Jab stock ka 12-year average RoE < 15% drop ho jaye ya continuous dividend distribution streak toot jaye. | stop: number nahi diya
- [new/yes] Dividend Discount Valuation Model Buy/Avoid Rule: Expected Total Return (g + Dividend Yield) ko Required Rate of Return (k) se evaluate karo; agar Expected Return (PAT/Dividend CAGR 'g' + Dividend Yield) >= Required Rate 'k' ho to current market price par buy karo, agar k > (g + Dividend Yield) ho to tabhi buy karo jab stock lower P/E multiple par mile. | entry: Buy when Expected Return (g + Dividend Yield) >= Required Return k, jahan g = expected long-term dividend/earnings growth rate aur Dividend Yield = Annual Dividend / Stock Price. | exit: Sell / Avoid when valuation expands to a level where Expected Return (g + Dividend Yield) drops significantly below required return k. | stop: number nahi diya
- [new/yes] High Dividend Payout Floor Valuation Rule: High Payout Ratio (> 40% se 80%) wali quality Blue Chip companies ke liye floor P/E = (Payout Ratio %) / (Upper Target Dividend Yield %), jaise 80% payout aur 3% upper yield band par theoretical floor P/E = 27x (80 / 3) banta hai; stock P/E is floor level ke paas aane par buy zone me mana jata hai. | entry: Buy when trailing P/E drops close to its theoretical floor P/E calculated as Payout % / Target Max Dividend Yield % (e.g. 80% payout with 3% yield floor = 27x P/E). | exit: Sell when P/E expands far above historical upper valuation range. | stop: number nahi diya
- [known/yes] 4-Factor Valuation Multi-bagger Screen: Un shares ko buy karo jo base year me simultaneously 4 valuation criteria clear karein: P/E < 10x, Price/Book < 1.5x, Price/Sales <= 1.0x, aur 5-Year Payback Ratio (Current Market Cap / 5-Year Cumulative PAT) < 1.0x. | entry: Buy stocks meeting all 4 conditions: trailing P/E < 10x, P/B < 1.5x, P/S <= 1.0x, and Payback Ratio < 1.0x. | exit: 5 saal ki holding horizon ke baad ya jab valuation metrics historical averages se upar nikal jaayein. | stop: number nahi diya
