5) Daily "FPI weather" checklist

Score each item +1 (supportive), 0 (neutral) or −1 (headwind), then add them up. A total of ≥+3 means fair weather; ≤−3 means storm. The thresholds are mine, so backtest them on your own data.

Flow momentum. 5-day and 20-day sums of NSE provisional FII cash flows, plus the streak length. Positive and rising scores +1.
Futures positioning. FII long % in index futures. Below 15% means crowded shorts and squeeze fuel (+1 contrarian). Rising through 30–40% means conviction (+1). Falling back under 20% while cash buying continues means a warning (−1).
Rupee. USDINR 10-day change. Rupee stronger by more than 0.5% is +1; weaker by more than 1% is −1.
US 10-year. 10-day change. Down 15bp or more is +1; up 15bp or more is −1. Add DXY 20-day change: above +2% is −1.
AI rotation spread. 5-day return of INDA minus the average of EWT and EWY. Above +2% is +1; below −2% is −1.
Risk. US VIX below 18 is +1; above 25 is −1.
Oil. Brent 10-day change above +8% is −1; below −8% is +1.
Calendar flag. Mark FOMC (9 Dec), RBI (2–4 Dec), MSCI/FTSE effective dates (30 Nov), Budget 2027 (expected 1 Feb), and large IPO/QIP/block deals. On those days, give the flow signal less weight.
