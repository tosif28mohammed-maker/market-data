1. UAZ8pZhCntU (Pranjal Kamra / Finology): types of equity mutual funds

SEBI's 2017 reclassification ended AMCs running several look-alike funds in one category. Each AMC may now have only one fund per category. A February 2026 change grouped all funds into five buckets: equity, debt, hybrid, life-cycle and "other" (index funds, ETFs, funds of funds).
Market-cap ranking: the top 100 companies are large cap, ranks 101–250 are mid cap, and everything else is small cap. SEBI has no micro or nano category.
What each fund type must hold:
Large cap: at least 80% in large caps.
Mid cap and small cap: at least 65% in their own segment.
Multi cap: at least 25% each in large, mid and small caps.
Flexi cap: at least 65% in equity, otherwise free.
Focused: no more than 30 stocks.
His views:
He likes focused funds (and plugs his Finology 30 product).
He dislikes value funds because "value" isn't defined, so they become a disguised extra flexi-cap fund.
ELSS matters less under the new tax regime, but its 3-year lock-in enforces discipline.
He dislikes sector and thematic funds. A narrow mandate defeats the point of paying an expert, and these funds crash hard when the sector falls.
