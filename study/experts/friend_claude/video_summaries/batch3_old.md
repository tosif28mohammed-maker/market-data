4. Why VWAP & Moving Averages Fail in Intraday (Trading Chanakya)

The indicators aren't the problem: In his backtest of about 7–8 years on 5-minute Nifty charts, VWAP and 9-SMA strategies were profitable before costs.
Costs are: At about ₹82–85 per ₹1 lakh round trip (STT, brokerage, GST and other charges), every strategy turned into a loss of up to around ₹24 lakh.
His fixes:
Trade high-momentum instruments like silver, where 5–6% daily candles are possible.
Take at most 1–2 trades a day.
Use a previous-day high/low breakout filter so you skip sideways days.

6. "It's Boring But It Works," Episode 2 (Dhan, presented by Mukul Chaudhary; this file had no title or channel line)

Setup: 5-minute chart with only R1/S1 pivots and the previous day's high/low. These are static levels that form an upper and a lower "bracket."
Rules:
Price above the upper bracket: long only.
Price below the lower bracket: short only.
Price opening inside the range: fade rejections at the brackets toward the opposite side, or target the 50% midpoint.
Entry is a pullback followed by a bullish or bearish engulfing candle. Stop goes just beyond the candle. Targets are 1:1 (move the stop to breakeven), then 1:2 and 1:3.
Skip big gap-up or gap-down days.
For index trades, use in-the-money options at least 100 points deep. For stocks, use cash or futures only, never options.
The "key input": Narrow bracket width points to a trending day. Wide width points to a sideways day, so reduce position size.
Discipline: Take at most about 30 trades in 20 sessions, and backtest for at least 6 months.
