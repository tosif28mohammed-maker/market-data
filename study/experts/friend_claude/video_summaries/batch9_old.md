1. 9fq1eBj_9Ok: A "boring" swing strategy with three indicators

Main idea: You make money from maths and statistics, not feelings. The presenter offers a rule-based system meant to keep emotion out of trading, and says he has used it for about 10 years.

Rules (long only):

Trend: three moving averages (SMA or EMA) in a 1:3:9 ratio. He uses 5/15/50, or 9/30/100 when he wants to be more conservative. They must already be in a bullish order. Longer averages give fewer, larger trades with a higher win rate. Shorter ones give more trades with smaller targets and smaller stops.
Entry trigger: Stochastic RSI gives a buy crossover from the oversold zone while the moving averages are bullish.
Momentum: ADX must be rising. Above 20 is better, but the slope is what counts.
Entry: above the high of the signal candle. Stop loss: low of the previous candle.
Exit: when Stochastic RSI becomes overbought (around 90–95) or its lines start to converge. Also exit if it drops straight back to oversold. There are time-based profit exits too: 3–4% within a week, 6–8% within a month, or 15–25% within a year.

Average returns he claims: daily chart 3–6%, weekly 7–10%, monthly 12–50%. Monthly carries more risk because the stops are wider. He says it works on stocks, indices and commodities, on any timeframe.

2. BjdeVTDnEhc: Bank Nifty 1-minute scalping with the 200 EMA (Dhan, Mukul Chaudhary)

Setup:

1-minute chart on Bank Nifty.
Trade at-the-money or about 100 points in-the-money options, with delta ideally above 0.6 (0.55 at minimum). Never trade out-of-the-money.
He chose Bank Nifty because it has monthly expiries, so time decay hurts less than in the Nifty or Sensex weeklies.

Rules:

Go long above the 200 EMA and short below it, only when price touches it. If price is far from the EMA, don't trade.
Entries: bullish engulfing or morning star for longs, bearish engulfing or evening star for shorts. Two- and three-candle patterns only.
The engulfing candle should have small wicks and cover at least 90% of the prior candle. The pattern must form within the next two bars. Skip very large candles because they mean wide stops, and don't chase.
Stop loss: beyond the pattern candle, or beyond the candle that crossed the EMA. Target: at least 1:2 or 1:3, trailing candle by candle until an opposite engulfing candle appears.
Move the stop to breakeven at about 1:1 to 1:1.5. His point is that protecting capital comes first.
Acceptable stop sizes: 40–50 points for Bank Nifty, 10–15 for Nifty, 60–70 for Sensex (when India VIX is around 12–13).

Expectations: 1–2 trades a day, about 15–20 a month, so patience is required. Backtest it for at least 6 months on Dhan's replay charts. The video also promotes charts.dhan.co.
