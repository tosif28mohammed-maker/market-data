4) Risk rules for holders
High PE (>60×). Growth stocks show an asymmetrically large negative price response to negative earnings surprises, and glamour stocks also show much larger negative drifts afterwards. Sensible defaults to test: 
repec
uwo
Trim 25–50% before results if the stock ran up more than 10% in the prior 2 weeks.
Hold the full position only if conviction is very high.
Moderate PE (15–40×). Holding through results has historically been fine: in Kundu & Banerjee, only non-performers saw a price drop after announcement. Don't sell just because results are coming. 
deutsche-digitale-bibliothek
After a miss, don't average down for 10 sessions. Bad-news drift is the most consistently reported effect in Indian studies.
Guidance and commentary matter as much as the numbers. A guidance cut outweighs an earnings beat. Wait for the conference call before acting, especially for after-hours releases.
Move sizes by PE/volatility bucket. I couldn't find published Indian figures. Compute them from your data: median absolute E return and 90th-percentile E return by PE bucket (<20, 20–40, 40–60, >60) × 60-day volatility tercile. Size positions so a 90th-percentile adverse move costs under 1% of the portfolio.
