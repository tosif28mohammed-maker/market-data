5) Failed-theme lessons
Railways (2024 darling). IRFC, RVNL and Titagarh fell more than 50% from their 2024 highs, even though the rail budget kept rising. DSIJ notes that expectations of higher spending and rising order books were aggressively priced in. Today RVNL's revenue grew only 1% and analyst targets sit about 20% below the price. Lesson: a growing budget is not the same as growing margins or new orders. 
whalesbook
dsij
Solar modules. 233 GW of module capacity has pushed factory utilisation down to 35–40%. Lesson: policy protection invites too much capacity. 
pv-magazine
Capital markets / F&O. Angel One's revenue fell 19% and profit 61% year-on-year in Q1 FY26. The Budget then raised STT (the securities transaction tax) to 0.05% on futures and 0.15% on options premium. Lesson: a regulator can wipe out a theme's earnings overnight. 
inc42
angelone
EMS governance. Kotak flagged mismatches in FY2025 related-party filings at Kaynes and its subsidiaries. Then in November 2026 the stock fell 20% on weak Q2 FY27 results, accounting adjustments, rising debt and weak cash flow. Lesson: a hot theme plus a premium PE magnifies any disclosure problem. 
business-standard
5paisa
Survivorship bias. Theme lists usually show only today's winners. Railway and solar names were on every 2024 "top picks" list.

The documented governance and pledge flags I found are Kaynes (disclosure issues), Kalpataru (8.2% promoter pledge) and Solar Industries (2.2% pledge).
