Spectacular Space Visuals: Russian Progress Spacecraft Docks With International Space Station

Watch spectacular visuals from outer space as Russia's Roscosmos Progress 97 cargo spacecraft successfully docks with the International Space Station (ISS) on November 26, 2026.

The uncrewed spacecraft, which launched aboard a Soyuz rocket from Kazakhstan on November 24, carried approximately three tons of food, fuel and essential supplies for the Expedition 75 crew.

NASA footage captures the spacecraft orbiting Earth, gradually approaching the space station and completing its precision docking with the Rassvet module at 7:08 AM EST (5:38 PM IST).
