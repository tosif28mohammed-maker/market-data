FAQs:

What are Advanced Trading Indicators?
Advanced Trading Indicators are technical analysis tools that may combine price, moving averages, volatility or other calculations to display market conditions differently from conventional indicators.

What is HalfTrend Indicator?
HalfTrend is a technical indicator used to study trend direction and potential changes in price behaviour through calculated trend levels.

What is Dhan AlphaTrend?
Dhan AlphaTrend is a trend-analysis indicator available on Dhan Charts that helps traders visualise bullish and bearish market conditions.

What is SSL Channel Indicator?
SSL Channel is a moving-average-based indicator commonly used to study trend direction, price transitions and dynamic reference levels.

What is UT Bot Indicator?
UT Bot is a trend-following indicator that generates buy and sell signals using price movement and volatility-based calculations, depending on its implementation.

Can UT Bot be used for intraday trading?
Yes. UT Bot can be applied to intraday charts, but its signals may behave differently across timeframes and market conditions.

What is Super Scalper Indicator?
Super Scalper is a technical indicator available on Dhan Charts that displays trading signals and configurable parameters such as bands and moving averages.

What are Golden Buy and Golden Sell signals?
These are signal labels displayed by the Super Scalper indicator. Their interpretation depends on the indicator's settings and underlying conditions.

Which indicators can be used on 1-minute charts?
Indicators such as AlphaTrend, UT Bot and Super Scalper can be studied on 1-minute charts, although shorter timeframes are often more sensitive to market noise.
