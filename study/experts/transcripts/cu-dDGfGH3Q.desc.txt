Tata Row Reaches Court: Tata Trusts Files Suit Against Tata Sons Over Voting Rights Mechanism

The Tata boardroom battle has now reached the courtroom. Tata Trusts have filed a suit seeking to invalidate the reappointment made in the September board meeting, challenging the use of the casting vote to settle a deadlock between trust nominee directors. The matter is set to be heard urgently.
