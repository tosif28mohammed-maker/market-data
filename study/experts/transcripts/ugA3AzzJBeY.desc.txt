Indonesia Gold Mine Tragedy: 8 Dead, 18 Trapped Underground After Heavy Rains Trigger Flooding

At least eight miners have died and 18 others are believed to be trapped after heavy rains flooded an unauthorised gold mine in Indonesia's West Java province. Rescuers are battling narrow passages, slippery terrain and poor visibility to reach miners trapped 100 metres underground. The tragedy highlights the dangers of illegal mining in Indonesia.
