Washington Wants The Market Even Lower | Weekly Roundup

Who still has an incentive to bid this market?

This week, we're joined by Cem Karsan and Noel Smith to unpack why Cem says the administration will clip the left tail but still wants the market lower after the midterms.

We explore why a 40 VIX is "nothing," his 20 to 30% path for the S&P, the gold-calls and oil-puts trade, and Waller's 50 basis point hike as credibility theater. Enjoy!
