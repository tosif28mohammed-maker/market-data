Tata Boardroom Battle Intensifies: Noel Tata Challenges Nov 30 Agenda, Raises Questions | Sources

The Tata Sons boardroom battle has taken a fresh turn ahead of the crucial November 30 board meeting, with Noel Tata raising a series of objections to the proposed agenda, sources say.

Noel Tata has sought priority discussions on SP Group financing and Tata Sons merger proposals, while questioning the sequencing of discussions on RBI correspondence and the potential listing of Tata Sons.

He has also sought detailed merger plans, supporting documents, legal opinions and greater clarity on regulatory compliance.

According to sources, Noel Tata has raised concerns over N Chandrasekaran's reappointment, the proposed EGM, the September 17 board meeting minutes and matters relating to corporate governance and disclosure of interest.

He has also sought a full audio-video recording of the upcoming November 30 board meeting.
