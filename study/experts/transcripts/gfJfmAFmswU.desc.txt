26/11 Memorial LIVE | Gateway Of India Lights Up In Tricolour | Mumbai Remembers Victims LIVE

Watch live from the Gateway of India in Mumbai as the National Security Guard (NSG) organises a special candlelight vigil and memorial ceremony marking the 18th anniversary of the 26/11 Mumbai terror attacks.

The #NEVEREVER memorial brings together citizens, survivors, martyrs' families and dignitaries to honour the lives lost and the bravery of India's security personnel.

The ceremony features candlelight tributes, special video screenings, a public pledge and the illumination of the Gateway of India in the national tricolour.

Date: November 26, 2026
Venue: Gateway of India, Mumbai
Event time: 7:30 PM IST onwards
