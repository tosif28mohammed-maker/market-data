Jensen Huang Returns To Nvidia Within 11 Days, But Can Nvidia's Board Be Trusted?

Just six days after accepting Jensen Huang's resignation, Nvidia's board unanimously reinstated him as CEO. But has Huang made himself unfireable? On The Big Question, experts discuss whether market pressure and US government intervention influenced the board's decision, raising concerns over corporate governance, succession planning and Nvidia's independence. The panel also examines unresolved national security concerns, circular financing within the AI ecosystem and the risks of excessive dependence on superstar tech founders.
