NSE Becomes World’s 3rd Most Valuable Listed Exchange! What Next for Investors?

NSE has become the world’s third-largest listed stock exchange operator by market capitalisation, overtaking the London Stock Exchange Group and Germany’s Deutsche Börse. But how did India’s National Stock Exchange become so valuable?

On November 26, 2026, NSE’s market capitalisation reached approximately ₹5.8 lakh crore, or $64.1 billion, placing it behind only CME Group and Intercontinental Exchange in the global ranking of listed exchange operators.

In this video, we explain the three major factors behind NSE’s valuation and the risks investors should understand.

1️⃣ NSE’s Market Dominance

NSE commands approximately 93% of India’s equity cash-market turnover and almost the entire equity futures market.

In FY26, approximately 51.18% of global equity derivatives contracts by volume were traded on NSE, highlighting its dominant position in derivatives trading.

2️⃣ Strong Profitability

NSE operates a technology-driven platform business where additional trading activity can generate revenue without a proportionate increase in operating expenses.

In FY26, NSE reported consolidated operating revenue of ₹16,601 crore and operating EBITDA of ₹11,098 crore, representing an operating EBITDA margin of approximately 67%.

In Q2 FY27, its operating EBITDA margin reached 76%.

3️⃣ India’s Growing Investor Base

As of September 2026, NSE had approximately 13.7 crore unique investors across more than 99% of India’s postal codes.

Growing investor participation, trading volumes, new listings and financial products create opportunities for the exchange.

However, there are important risks.

Transaction charges account for a significant share of NSE’s income. Weekly Nifty index options alone contributed approximately 42% of operating revenue in Q2 FY27.

Changes in derivatives regulations, trading activity or transaction charges could affect future earnings.

NSE shares have also risen significantly since listing, making valuation expectations an important consideration for investors.

What should NSE investors monitor next?

✅ Trading volume growth and market share

✅ Revenue diversification beyond transaction charges

✅ Regulatory changes affecting derivatives

✅ Operating margins and earnings sustainability

Can NSE maintain its global position and continue growing, or are future expectations already reflected in its share price?
