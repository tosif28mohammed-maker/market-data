18 Years On, Mumbai Remembers The Heroes And Victims Of The 26/11 Terror Attacks

Eighteen years after the tragic 26/11 Mumbai terror attacks, the city pays tribute to the innocent victims and honours the courage and sacrifice of the brave heroes who defended Mumbai.

We remember those we lost, salute our security personnel and stand in solidarity with the survivors and their families.

26/11 — A day Mumbai will never forget.
