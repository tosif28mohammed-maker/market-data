Trump Places His Hand On A Turkey And Grants It A Full Presidential Pardon | WATCH

US President Donald Trump and First Lady Melania Trump participate in the annual Thanksgiving turkey pardon ceremony at the White House in Washington, DC.

This year's turkeys, named Freedom and Feathers, were raised on a farm in West Virginia. The presidential turkey pardon is a longstanding American tradition marking the Thanksgiving holiday, where selected turkeys are spared from the Thanksgiving dinner table.
