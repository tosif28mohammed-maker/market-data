Citi India Decodes Auto Stocks: Where Are the Biggest Opportunities Now?

Where are the biggest opportunities in India's auto sector as the GST-led demand boost begins to fade? Citi India shares its outlook on the automobile industry, preferred segments and the top investment opportunities for investors.

With vehicle demand normalising and commodity costs putting pressure on gross margins, how much upside is left in auto stocks? We discuss the outlook for passenger vehicles and two-wheelers, margin recovery, upcoming launches and the challenges facing automakers.

The discussion also examines Citi India's preferred auto stocks, the earnings outlook for FY27 and FY28, and whether Indian companies can sustain 15-16% earnings growth.
