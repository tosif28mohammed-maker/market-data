Sara Duterte Impeachment Hearing Day 46 LIVE | Philippines VP Senate Proceedings | Nov 23 LIVE

Philippines Vice President Sara Duterte's impeachment trial faces a fresh scheduling twist after Day 45 of the Senate proceedings concluded on November 20, 2026.

The Senate impeachment court is now expected to resume hearings on December 1 after the defense raised concerns about witness availability.

The latest developments come as Duterte's legal team continues to challenge allegations involving unexplained wealth, financial declarations and Cale88 Foods Corporation, a business linked to her husband, Manases Carpio.

The proceedings have also seen fresh controversy over remarks by Office of the Vice President spokesperson Paolo Panelo concerning senator-judges.

Prosecutors have focused their impeachment case on allegations of unexplained wealth involving approximately ₱817 million. Duterte denies wrongdoing and disputes the allegations.
