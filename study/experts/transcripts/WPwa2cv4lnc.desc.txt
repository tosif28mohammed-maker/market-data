'80% Pay Cut, Relocate Or Quit': IBM's Ultimatum To H-1B Workers

IBM is reportedly giving employees on its US visa and green card sponsorship programme a difficult choice: take a pay cut and relocate abroad, or leave the company.

The decision, which reportedly affects most positions within IBM Consulting, comes as the Trump administration tightens immigration rules and increases scrutiny of foreign-worker hiring in the United States.

According to Bloomberg, affected employees have been asked to relocate to positions outside the US or leave the company by December 31, with three months of severance reportedly being offered.

India appears to be a major relocation destination. Of the 1,278 consulting vacancies IBM listed outside the US on November 19, as many as 1,125 were in India.

For Indian professionals working in the US, this raises serious questions about job security, salaries, H-1B sponsorship and the future of employment-based immigration.

Is this an IBM-specific restructuring decision, or a sign of a bigger shift in America's approach to foreign talent?
