Kearney Flags India's Next Economic Challenge: IT Disruption, China+1 & US Tariffs

Can India's traditional IT outsourcing model survive the AI revolution? In an exclusive conversation with NDTV Profit, Kearney's leadership warns that manpower-based IT outsourcing models are no longer sustainable and that failure to adapt could become one of the Indian economy's biggest challenges.

The leadership also explains why the China+1 strategy has been underwhelming for India, how global supply chains are being reshaped by geopolitical tensions, and why improving productivity is critical to India's global competitiveness.

From rising AI valuations and uncertainty over investment returns to changing global trade patterns, industrial policies and the resilience of the Indian economy, Kearney decodes the biggest business challenges heading into 2027. The leadership also identifies AI infrastructure, data centres and cloud infrastructure as promising investment opportunities for India.
