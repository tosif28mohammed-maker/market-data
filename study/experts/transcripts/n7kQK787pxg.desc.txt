Index Funds vs Active Mutual Funds | Finology Academy of Mutual Funds Ep 7

Welcome to the Finology Academy of Mutual Funds! 

In this seventh episode, we tackle one of the biggest debates in personal finance: Active vs. Passive Investing, and uncover why roughly 85% of actively managed large-cap funds fail to beat their benchmark index, and when paying for an active fund manager still makes sense.

Key concepts explained in this episode:

• What is the core difference between Active Mutual Funds (trying to beat the market) and Passive Index Funds & ETFs (mirroring the market)?

• Why do ~84–85% of actively managed large-cap funds in India underperform their benchmark over 5-year periods according to the 2026 SPIVA India Scorecard?

• How do higher Expense Ratios, market efficiency, and large fund sizes quietly eat away at an active fund manager's returns over 15–20 years?

• What is the difference between an Index Mutual Fund and an Exchange-Traded Fund (ETF), and when do you need a Demat account?

•How do Tracking Error and Tracking Difference help you pick the best Index Fund or ETF on the same benchmark?

• Where do active fund managers still have an edge (such as Mid Cap and Small Cap categories), and how can you combine both using a Core-Satellite portfolio strategy?
