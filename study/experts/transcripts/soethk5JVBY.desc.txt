'Again Sticking My Neck Out On These...' Feroze Azeez Shares His Top Bets For India Markets

What gave Feroze Azeez the conviction to borrow money and invest in Indian equities when markets were under pressure? The Joint CEO of Anand Rathi Wealth reveals the data and investment strategy behind his leveraged bets and explains why he remains bullish despite the sharp market recovery.

Azeez discusses India's earnings growth outlook, mutual fund performance, institutional liquidity and the potential to generate 12–14% annualised returns over a three-year investment horizon. He also explains why he expects F&O stocks to outperform non-F&O stocks, remains bullish on FMCG and sees the Nifty Consumer Durables index outperforming the benchmark by around four percentage points annually over the next two years.
