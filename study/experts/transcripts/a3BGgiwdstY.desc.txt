Gold Price Crash 2026: Why are gold prices falling, and can gold recover after a correction of approximately 30% from its January peak?

In this video, we explain the three major reasons behind the gold price decline, what could support a recovery, and the key factors investors should monitor before making an investment decision.

Why is gold falling in 2026?

1️⃣ US Interest Rates

At the beginning of 2026, investors expected the US Federal Reserve to cut interest rates. However, resilient economic activity and persistent inflation have increased expectations of tighter monetary policy.

Higher interest rates and bond yields can reduce the relative attractiveness of gold because it does not generate interest income.

2️⃣ Changes in Global Trade Uncertainty

US tariff policies previously encouraged investors to seek safe-haven assets such as gold. Progress in trade negotiations and moderation in certain trade tensions have since influenced demand for safe-haven investments.

3️⃣ Geopolitical Tensions and Crude Oil

Changes in Middle East geopolitical risks and crude oil prices have affected inflation expectations and demand for gold as a safe-haven asset.

But could gold prices recover?

Central bank gold purchases remain an important source of long-term demand. Many central banks use gold to diversify their international reserves, providing a potential structural source of support.

Some fund houses are also evaluating opportunities following the correction.

DSP Mutual Fund has outlined a systematic gold accumulation strategy through its Multi Asset Omni Fund of Funds, with purchases at predefined price levels rather than attempting to predict the market bottom.
