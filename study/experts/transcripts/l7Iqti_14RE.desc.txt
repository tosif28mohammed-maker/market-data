How China Prepared Pakistani Astronaut Zeeshan Ali For Shenzhou-24 Mission

Pakistani astronaut Muhammad Zeeshan Ali has become the first foreign astronaut to board China's Tiangong space station, according to CCTV. Pakistan's astronaut programme director Amer Gilani explains how China trained the former Pakistan Air Force pilot for spaceflight, emergency response and scientific experiments.

Ali will participate in four scientific experiments during the Shenzhou-24 mission.
