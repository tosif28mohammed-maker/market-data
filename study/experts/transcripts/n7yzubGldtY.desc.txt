After Two Years In India, Sheikh Hasina Announces Plan To Return To Bangladesh

Ousted Bangladesh Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina plans to return home on December 16 after more than two years in exile in India, Reuters reported, citing her spokesperson. The proposed return coincides with Bangladesh's Victory Day and comes amid political and legal tensions surrounding the banned Awami League.
