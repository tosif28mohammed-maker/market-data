Watch: India CALLS OUT Global AI Divide At UN, Warns Of A New Threat In Warfare

India flagged the risks posed by artificial intelligence in armed conflicts, highlighting concerns over autonomous weapons, terrorist misuse of emerging technologies and manipulated data at the United Nations Security Council.

India also raised concerns over technology denial, restrictive export controls and the exclusion of Global South countries from AI governance, while renewing its call for UNSC reforms.
