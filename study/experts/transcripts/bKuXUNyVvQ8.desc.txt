The Bigger Story Behind Trump's 25% Tariff Proposal And India's Never-Ending Trade Negotiations

Just when India and the United States appeared to be approaching a trade agreement, Donald Trump’s administration has unveiled another tariff proposal.

Under the proposed framework, tariffs on Indian imports could rise to 25%, potentially falling to 17% if a bilateral trade agreement is signed.

But the bigger question isn't just about tariff rates. It's about the uncertainty surrounding America's trade policy, Trump's push to bring manufacturing back to the United States, and the future of India's economic relationship with Washington.

NDTV Profit examines the legal battles behind Trump's tariff strategy, India's position against competing export economies, and why the much-awaited India-US trade deal remains elusive.
