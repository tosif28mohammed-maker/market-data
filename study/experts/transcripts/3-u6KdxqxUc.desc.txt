America's Economy Is Growing At 4.3%. So Why Is Wall Street Panicking?| US Markets Explained

Wall Street is facing a triple threat as fresh tariff announcements, hotter-than-expected US inflation data and growing concerns over the sustainability of the artificial intelligence spending boom fuel market volatility.

The Nasdaq has flirted with bear market territory, while US core PCE inflation stands at 3.3% and headline inflation at 3.8%, intensifying concerns over the Federal Reserve's next interest rate decision.

Despite US Q3 GDP growth being revised to 4.3%, investors remain concerned about the economy's growing dependence on AI investment.

Reuters US Economics Editor Dan Burns explains what's driving the market turmoil, why Donald Trump's intervention at Nvidia has failed to fully reassure investors and what persistent inflation could mean for the Federal Reserve.
