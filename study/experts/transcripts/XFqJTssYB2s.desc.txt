Trump Tariffs: Japan REJECTS US Overcapacity Claims, Reminds Washington Of Trade Deal

Japan has expressed displeasure over the Trump administration's announcement of fresh tariff measures targeting 14 major trading partners. Japanese Chief Cabinet Secretary Minoru Kihara rejected allegations of structural industrial overcapacity and reminded Washington of the commitments made under the previous Japan-US trade agreement.
