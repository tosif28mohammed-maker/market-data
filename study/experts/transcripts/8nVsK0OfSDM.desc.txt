Gold & Silver Prices Bounce Back After 5 Days! Will the Rally Last?

Gold and Silver Price Recovery: After five consecutive sessions of losses, gold and silver prices rebounded sharply on November 23, 2026. But is this the beginning of a fresh rally or just a temporary recovery?

Gold futures on the Multi Commodity Exchange (MCX) rose more than 2%, while silver futures gained approximately ₹3,600 per kg during the trading session.

The recovery follows a difficult week for precious metals, with international gold prices falling more than 9%.

So, why are gold and silver prices rising again?

Here are 3 important factors behind the rebound:

1️⃣ Oversold Conditions: Following a sharp correction, fresh buying at lower prices can support a short-term recovery in gold and silver.

2️⃣ Geopolitical Uncertainty: Rising tensions in the Middle East and concerns over disruptions around the Strait of Hormuz can increase safe-haven demand for gold.

3️⃣ Upcoming US Inflation Data: The US PCE inflation report scheduled for November 25 could influence expectations for the Federal Reserve's December interest-rate decision.

However, higher interest-rate expectations remain a significant concern.

Gold does not generate regular interest income. When US interest rates and Treasury yields rise, interest-bearing investments can become more attractive. A stronger US dollar can also put pressure on precious metal prices.

What should gold and silver investors monitor next?

✅ US PCE inflation data

✅ Federal Reserve interest-rate expectations

✅ US Treasury yields and dollar movements

✅ Middle East geopolitical developments
