Why Is Europe's Fastest Growing Economy Struggling To Survive?

Spain is emerging as one of Europe's biggest economic success stories, growing at more than twice the pace of the eurozone. Employment is rising, tourism is booming and minimum wages have increased significantly. Yet, ordinary Spaniards are struggling with unaffordable housing, rising rents, stagnant real wages and soaring grocery bills.

As Spain heads into a snap election on November 29, we break down the country's economic paradox through data and explore why strong GDP growth isn't necessarily translating into greater financial prosperity for its citizens.
