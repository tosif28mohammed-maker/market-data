How US Technology Found Its Way Into China's Warplanes

Did the West help China build the very military capabilities it is now trying to contain?

From precision machine tools and aircraft manufacturing technology to aerospace engineering know-how, Western companies have spent decades working with China's aviation industry. A US congressional report now alleges that several commercial partnerships and technology transfers may have strengthened China's military aerospace capabilities.

We look at how American and European companies became part of China's aerospace story, why seemingly legitimate business deals raised national security concerns, and why Washington is now considering tougher restrictions on technology transfers and investments in China.
