Titan's ₹1,000 Crore Perfume Ambition: SKINN & Fastrack's Big Growth Plan | Manish Gupta

Titan is betting big on India's rapidly growing fragrance market with an ambitious ₹1,000 crore business target by FY30.

In an exclusive conversation with NDTV Profit's Riddhima Bhatnagar, Manish Gupta, CEO – Fragrance & Fashion Accessories at Titan, discusses the launch of SKINN's Signature Collection, its premiumisation strategy, Fastrack's growing perfume business and the company's plans to reach eight million fragrance consumers.

He also breaks down India's ₹4,500–5,000 crore perfume market, evolving consumer preferences, distribution expansion and Titan's strategy to capture a larger share of the fragrance industry.
