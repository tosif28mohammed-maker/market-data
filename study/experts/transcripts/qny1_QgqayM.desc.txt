Beyond The AI Trade: Why FIIs Are Betting On Indian Markets Again

Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) are returning to Indian markets, raising hopes of a fresh wave of foreign capital inflows. But what's driving this renewed interest in India?

After remaining significantly underweight on Indian equities, global investors are beginning to reassess their India exposure. Resilient macroeconomic fundamentals, improving corporate earnings and stronger earnings visibility are making Indian markets attractive again.
