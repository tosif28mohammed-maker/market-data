‘We Could Not Respond In 2008’: Air Chief Marshal On 26/11, Operation Sindoor & India's New Normal

On the 18th anniversary of the 26/11 Mumbai terror attacks, Air Chief Marshal Ashutosh Dixit reflects on how India's response to terrorism has evolved since 2008.

Speaking in Mumbai, he highlights Operation Sindoor, India's military preparedness and its changing deterrence strategy. He also reiterates Prime Minister Narendra Modi's stated position on terrorism, dialogue and those supporting terrorist activities.
