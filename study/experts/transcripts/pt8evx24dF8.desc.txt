If Deepam Is Not Lit Tomorrow: J Sai Deepak Warns CM Vijay Govt Of Bigger Consequences

Will the Deepam finally be lit at Thiruparankundram's Deepathoon on November 28?

In an interview with NDTV, advocate J Sai Deepak issues a sharp warning to Tamil Nadu Chief Minister Vijay's government against any further obstruction of the lamp-lighting ceremony ordered by the Supreme Court.

Deepak argues that any attempt to prevent the ceremony on law-and-order grounds could invite more serious legal consequences. He also explains the Supreme Court's contempt powers under Article 129, counters arguments surrounding the breakdown of India's federal structure and questions the conduct of the state administration.
