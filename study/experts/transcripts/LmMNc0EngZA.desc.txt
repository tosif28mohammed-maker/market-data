Pakistan, Turkey & Saudi Arabia Eye Expansion Of Mecca Defence Pact: Who Could Join Next?

Pakistan, Saudi Arabia and Turkey have discussed a roadmap for admitting potential new members into the Mecca Defence Alliance during a meeting in Islamabad. The three nations reaffirmed their collective defence commitment, condemned Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia and agreed to strengthen military and technological cooperation.
