Can You Inherit Crypto? Understanding Your Legal Rights In India

The Madras High Court's recognition of cryptocurrency as a form of property under Indian law could have significant implications for investors. But what does this mean for ownership rights, custody of digital assets and legal recourse in case of disputes? In this episode of Crypto Minutes, powered by CoinSwitch, Nawad Singh, Associate Director – Legal at CoinSwitch, explains how crypto assets could be treated in divorce proceedings, inheritance disputes and creditor claims. He also discusses the importance of ownership records, asset valuation, estate planning, nominee details and safeguarding private keys. As crypto becomes an increasingly important part of financial portfolios, understanding the legal rights and responsibilities of investors is crucial.
