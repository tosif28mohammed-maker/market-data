Tata Trusts Rift Deepens As Vijay Singh Seeks Probe Into Proposed Litigation Against Tata Sons

The Tata Trusts rift deepens as Vice-Chairman Vijay Singh seeks an urgent Charity Commissioner probe into the Sir Dorabji Tata Trust's (SDTT) proposed litigation against Tata Sons and certain board members.

Singh warns that the proposed injunction could obstruct Tata Sons' RBI-mandated listing and raises concerns over the potential use of charitable funds for litigation. He has sought scrutiny of trustee votes, meeting minutes, legal opinions and litigation expenses, along with a hearing before November 30.
