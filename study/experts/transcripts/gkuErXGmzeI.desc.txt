Profit vs Cash Flow: What’s the Difference?

What is a Cash Flow Statement, and why is it important in fundamental analysis? In this video, we explain the difference between net profit and actual cash flow, how to analyse Cash Flow from Operations (CFO), and how the cash conversion ratio helps investors assess a company's earnings quality and financial health. Learn how negative operating cash flow, rising inventory, delayed receivables and weak cash generation can signal potential financial risks. We also explain why comparing operating cash flow with net profit over five years can provide better insights into a company's financial performance. This video is ideal for stock market beginners looking to understand financial statements and improve their fundamental analysis skills.
