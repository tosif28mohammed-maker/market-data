LIVE: NATO Chief Warns Of Russia Threat, Urges Europe To Boost Defence Spending | Mark Rutte Live

NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte addresses the NATO Parliamentary Assembly in Berlin, warning that Europe's heavy dependence on the US military is not sustainable.

Rutte calls on European allies to take greater responsibility for their own security, accelerate defence spending and strengthen military production as Russia continues its war against Ukraine.

The NATO chief also discusses the growing security threats posed by Russia, China's expanding military influence, support for Ukraine and the need to boost production of ammunition, air defence systems and other critical military equipment.
