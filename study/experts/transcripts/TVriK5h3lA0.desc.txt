US Braces For Possible Airline Attacks As Al-Qaeda Threat Warning Emerges

The FBI and US Department of Homeland Security have issued a warning to the aviation industry over a potential Al-Qaeda-inspired terror threat, urging airlines and airport authorities to remain on high alert ahead of the Thanksgiving travel rush.

An intelligence assessment reportedly warns that Al-Qaeda supporters are encouraging attacks targeting commercial aviation, praising a September cockpit attack attempt aboard a flydubai flight bound for Tel Aviv and calling for similar attacks against US and Israeli targets.

The warning comes as millions of Americans prepare to travel for Thanksgiving. AAA estimates that around 81.5 million Americans will travel at least 50 miles from home between November 24 and November 30, with millions expected to fly.

US authorities, however, stress that there is no specific, credible threat at this time. But the warning highlights growing concerns over individuals inspired by extremist groups and the evolving threat to commercial aviation.
