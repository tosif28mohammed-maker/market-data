Can SIPs really make you wealthy? How much should you invest, when should you increase your SIP, and how do you know when it's time to book profits?

In this episode, we sit down with Nilesh Shah, Group President and Managing Director of Kotak Mahindra AMC, for an insightful conversation on how retail Indian investors can build long-term wealth. Drawing on decades of experience in the markets, he explains why SIPs are just the starting point of wealth creation and what investors need to do beyond them.

From building a diversified multi-asset portfolio to identifying investment opportunities, managing risk and knowing when to exit, Nilesh shares practical frameworks that investors can use across different market cycles.
