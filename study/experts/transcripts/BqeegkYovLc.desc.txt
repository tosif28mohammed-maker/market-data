BREAKING: Tata Sons Files Caveats In Bombay HC And Supreme Court

Tata Sons has filed caveats in the Bombay High Court and the Supreme Court against Tata Trusts. The move comes after the High Court on November 27 allowed the company's November 30 board meeting to go ahead.

Legal experts say the Trusts could now take the dispute to the Supreme Court.
