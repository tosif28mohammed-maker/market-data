Power Sector Boom: Can these 2 stocks sustain their growth?

India's power sector is witnessing strong growth, but are power equipment stocks like Hitachi Energy India and GE Vernova T&D India worth tracking? In this video, we analyse the latest Q2 FY27 results of Hitachi Energy India and GE Vernova T&D India, where order inflows grew by 86% and 52%, respectively. We explore how India's ₹9 lakh crore power transmission investment plan, renewable energy expansion, and rising demand for transformers, substations, switchgear, and HVDC systems are creating opportunities for power transmission and distribution companies. We also compare revenue growth, order books, profit margins, operating cash flow, working capital requirements, and stock valuations to understand the financial performance of these two companies. Despite strong order inflows and revenue growth, high P/E ratios and weak cash conversion remain important factors for investors to evaluate. Watch this fundamental analysis to understand the growth potential, business risks, and key financial metrics to track in India's power equipment sector and electrical infrastructure stocks.
