Can These 3 Railway Stocks Make a Comeback?

Can railway stocks make a comeback after falling over 60% from their all-time highs? With Indian Railways' ₹2.93 lakh crore FY27 capex and fresh wagon orders worth ₹3,925 crore, we analyse three major railway stocks – Jupiter Wagons, Titagarh Rail Systems and Texmaco Rail & Engineering. This video compares their order books, Q2 FY27 results, revenue growth, P/E ratios and valuations to understand the potential recovery in India's railway sector. We also discuss the key factors investors should track, including order execution, operating margins and cash flow, before assessing their future growth prospects.
